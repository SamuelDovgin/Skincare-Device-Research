## What remains uncertain

No current-platform head-to-head study establishes a universal best non-laser device or a common Asian preference. Many clinical studies are small, uncontrolled, use bundles or cannot be mapped to retail SKUs. Complete funding/conflict assessment is still missing for several abstract-only records. Ultrasound/histologic findings are not interchangeable with visible outcomes.

The most useful next research is exact-model independent comparison with matched skincare, blinded assessment, longer follow-up, adherence and adverse events. A tissue endpoint can strengthen the mechanism case but must be interpreted beside meaningful visible benefit. Broader Asia coverage needs a dedicated regional census.
