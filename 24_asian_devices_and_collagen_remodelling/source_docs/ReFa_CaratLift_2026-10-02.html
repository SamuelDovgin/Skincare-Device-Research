Safety Precautions ＋

Please read the instruction manual carefully before use and follow all safety guidelines.

○ Never use this product together with the following types of medical electrical equipment. Doing so may cause the equipment to malfunction or lead to serious injury.

  • - Implanted medical electrical equipment, such as pacemakers
  • - Life-support medical devices, such as heart-lung machines
  • - Wearable medical electrical equipment, such as electrocardiographs (EKGs)
  • Note: Those using such medical devices must not touch anyone who is using the product.

○ Those who fall under any of the categories below must not use this product. Using the product in these conditions may lead to accidents or issues with the skin or body.

  • - Those with metal allergies
  • - Those with sensory impairments or skin problems (such as infections, inflammation, or wounds)
  • - Those with bleeding disorders (e.g., hemophilia)
  • - Those who are feeling unwell
  • - Those who are prone to bruising
  • - Those whose doctors have advised against the use of EMS devices
  • - Those who are pregnant or postpartum.
  • - Those with burns, blisters, wounds, cuts, irritated skin, skin infections, or any other abnormalities on or around the area where the roller will be used
  • - Young children, or those who are unable to understand this instruction manual
  • - Those under the influence of alcohol
  • - Those with a fever (38°C or higher)

○ Those who fall under any of the categories below should consult a physician before using this product. Using the product in these conditions may lead to accidents or issues with the skin or body.

  • - Those with malignant tumors
  • - Those who have or are suspected of having heart disease (e.g., those who experience chest discomfort such as pain or palpitations)
  • - Those who have a medical history of heart disease
  • - Those with sensory abnormalities caused by a significant peripheral circulatory disorder (e.g., diabetes)
  • - Those with cranial nerve-related conditions (e.g., epilepsy)
  • - Those with bone disorders (e.g., osteoporosis)
  • - Those with acute or painful conditions (e.g., spinal fractures, sprains, or muscle strains)
  • - Those with orthopedic conditions (e.g., spinal stenosis, herniated disc, gout, sciatica)
  • - Those with abnormal blood pressure, blood disorders or blood clots, or those suspected of having such conditions
  • - Those who have a wound or have undergone surgery on or around the area where the roller is used
  • - Those who are receiving nursing care or require support to do daily tasks
  • - Those who have metal implants (e.g., surgical screws) in their body
  • - Those with disorders of the circulatory system
  • - Those who have a tattoo on or around the area where the roller is used
  • - Those who are prone to allergies
  • - Those who require bed rest
  • - Those experiencing any physical abnormalities
  • - Those who have been instructed by a doctor not to exercise
  • - Those with sensitive skin
  • - Those who have recently undergone surgery
  • - Those who have received eye treatment
  • - Those who have surgical staples, clips, etc.
  • - Those who have implants
  • - Those undergoing dental or orthodontic treatment
  • - Those who wear partial dentures
  • - Those who are taking medication
  • - Those currently receiving treatment from a medical institution
  • Note: Even if none of the above conditions apply, please consult a doctor if you have any concerns about using the product.

○ Do not use the product on the following areas of the body. Doing so may result in accidents or skin or body problems.

  • - Eyes, lips, throat
  • - Mucous membranes and broken skin
  • - Areas with swelling, acne, or pus-forming infections
  • - Areas with abnormal skin conditions such as severe sunburn
  • - Areas that have undergone hair removal on the same day
  • - Areas that have undergone cosmetic surgery
  • - Bony areas such as the spine
  • - Areas with implants or metal implants (screws, plates, metal threads, etc.), resins, or silicone
  • - Thin areas such as fingers and Achilles tendons
  • Note: Do not use the product on any areas other than those specified in this manual or the quick guide.

○ Do not use face mode or body mode on the head, forehead, temples, areas around the eyes, neck, or chest near the heart. Doing so may cause accidents or injuries.

○ Do not use body mode on your face. Doing so may cause accidents or injuries.

Warning: When using EMS for face or body care, be sure to select the correct mode and follow the proper method of use for each area.

○ The sensation of EMS stimulation may vary depending on the moisture content of the skin (such as the amount of gel or lotion applied, or the amount of sweat on the skin). Please be aware that the sensation may become stronger when moving to a different area or when applying additional gel or lotion during use.

○ When using EMS for face or body care, do not use it on the same area for more than 10 minutes per day. Do not use the product while performing other tasks.

○ Please do not do any of the following. Doing so may cause accidents or injuries.

  • - Using the product on anyone other than yourself
  • - Switching modes or increasing the intensity level without the user’s consent
  • - Disassembling, modifying, or attempting to repair the product
  • - Placing the product in a microwave or pressure cooker
  • - Using the product near open flames

○ Do not use the product for any purpose other than its intended use.

○ The product is not waterproof. Do not pour water on it or immerse it in water. Doing so may cause fires, electric shock, or product damage.

Warning: Do not use the product in a bathtub, shower, or any container filled with water.

○ Do not use the product in a sauna. Doing so may cause burns or deform or damage its parts.

○ The product is for indoor use only. Do not store or use it outdoors.

○ Do not cool or warm the product before use. Doing so may cause frostbite or burns, or deform or damage its parts.

○ Do not drop the product, hit it against objects, or subject it to strong impacts. Doing so may damage the product. Continuing to use it when it is damaged may cause accidents or injuries.

○ Do not allow the roller to come into contact with USB cables or metal objects. Doing so may cause electric shocks.

○ Do not use the product with electronic devices or beauty devices other than those recommended by our company. Doing so may cause discomfort or adverse effects on your health.

○ Do not use the product while driving or operating a motor vehicle.

○ Do not use the product on young children or pets. Do not allow young children or pets to play with it. Doing so may cause accidents or damage the product.

○ Do not use the products if your hands have lotion or cream on them. Doing so may cause the product to slip and fall, which may result in injury.

○ Check the product for scratches or deformities before use. Continuing to use the product when damaged may cause accidents or injury.

○ When using the product, please tie your hair up or secure it with a hair band. Hair may become caught in the product, causing injury.

○ Remove any accessories (necklaces, earrings, rings, etc.), masks, and metal-framed glasses when using the product. Not doing so may cause accidents or injuries, or may damage the product.

○ When using the product for the first time, or when changing the gel, lotion, or any other product used together with it, always perform a patch test to ensure that no adverse reactions occur.

○ When first using the roller, use it gently, being aware of the amount of pressure applied and monitoring the condition of your skin. Applying excessive pressure may damage your skin or the product . Light pressure is sufficient to feel the sensation.

○ When first using EMS, start at a low level. Once you become accustomed to the sensation, gradually increase the level.

○ If you experience pain in your teeth while using the product, move the roller to an area within the usable range where you feel less pain, or lower the intensity level..

○ If you experience pain or discomfort during or after use, or if any abnormalities arise such as roughness, rashes, redness, itchiness, burning, or inflammation of the skin, stop using the product and consult a doctor. Continuing to use the product despite having an abnormal reaction to it may aggravate the symptoms. If you have any concerns, test the product on an inconspicuous area before using it to ensure that your skin will not react adversely.

○ Always turn off the power after use.

○ If the product becomes deformed or damaged, emits an unusual odor or noise, or becomes abnormally hot during charging or use, immediately stop charging or using it, turn it off, and contact the MTG Customer Service Center.
