        How the overall score works

        Overall fit combines protection (27%), selected-goal coverage (25%), recovery (18%), health habits (15%), and tolerability (15%), then subtracts a small complexity penalty. Strong conflicts—such as Tria plus adapalene on one day—lower tolerability. Optional products have capped credit, so adding every serum cannot create a perfect score. These are illustrative, evidence-informed weights, not a validated clinical scale.
