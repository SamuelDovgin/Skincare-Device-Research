            How the overall score works

            Overall fit combines protection (27%), selected-goal coverage (25%), recovery (18%), health habits (15%), and tolerability (15%), then subtracts a small complexity penalty. Strong conflicts—such as Tria plus adapalene on one day—lower tolerability. The concern chips show why a routine that covers acne well may still be weak for pigment, recovery, or health habits. Optional products have capped credit, so adding every serum cannot create a perfect score. A score of 100 is a theoretical routine-fit ceiling, not a predicted clinical result.
