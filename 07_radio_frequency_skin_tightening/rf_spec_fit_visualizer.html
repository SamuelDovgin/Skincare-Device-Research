The collagen chain: what is established?

This is an evidence map, not a dose simulation. A skin sensor cannot fill in unknown dermal temperature, and photos or thickness cannot become a collagen-growth percentage.

The right specifications to prioritize

1. Repeatable RF delivery

Exact hardware, true RF output on stated loads, waveform/duty cycle, electrode area/spacing and validated coupling. Charger watts and number of modes are insufficient.

2. Temperature and contact control

Sensor location/accuracy, numeric limit, shutdown/restart, fault response and full-contact behavior. A roughly 40–42°C skin-control record is a device guardrail, not a collagen target to chase.

3. Human evidence on the sold device

Comparator, blinded endpoints, sample and withdrawals, adverse events, sponsorship, usable regimen and durability. A brand-family or mouse study does not validate every retail generation.

4. Honest collagen measurement

Human biopsy/staining, spectral estimates, ultrasound thickness, elasticity and wrinkle photographs are different endpoints. No percentage is calculated from these inputs.
