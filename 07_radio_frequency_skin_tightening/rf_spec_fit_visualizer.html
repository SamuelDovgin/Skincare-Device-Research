How to read the visualizer

Fit score. For each selected device, the tool calculates 100 × Σ(weight × rating) ÷ (4 × Σ(active weights)). The input is a 1–4 grade for each documentation dimension, not a measured device performance value. Ratings are based on the sources linked in each model card.

Evidence coverage. Six decision fields are each marked documented, partly documented, not located, or unverified. Present counts as 1, partial as 0.5, and the last two as 0; coverage is the sum divided by six. A high fit score with weak coverage cannot enter the ranked shortlist.

Missing is not zero performance. A missing temperature limit, clinical study or output test is a research gap. The coverage strip makes that gap visible; it never represents a device measurement or negative clinical result.

No value-for-money ratio. The models have different countries, protocols, consumables, evidence and outcome endpoints. Confirm landed price, gel or interface cost, warranty, service and returns in your region before purchase.
