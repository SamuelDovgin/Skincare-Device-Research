# RF Product Lineup and Efficacy-Informed Tier List

*Updated October 2, 2026. Editorial recommendations based on human outcomes, exact-model transfer, thermal controls and support. Targeted research synthesis, not an exhaustive systematic review. Tiers are judgments under uncertainty, not measured differences in treatment effect.*

## My actual recommendation

**For a current home device for mild facial wrinkles, I would investigate CurrentBody Skin RF ST030 first. For direct subject-device human evidence, original NEWA is the strongest anchor.** CurrentBody's controls and current U.S. identity make it my practical first shortlist; its FDA summary explicitly reports no new clinical testing on the subject device. It has not been shown to outperform NEWA. [[1]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/DEN150005.pdf)[[2]](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf23/K232424.pdf)

**There is no S-tier home RF device.** No reviewed current product combines convincing replicated sham-controlled trials, strong durability and direct human collagen confirmation. Expect potentially modest wrinkle/skin-quality improvement, not a large lift or a promised percentage of new collagen. I would not pay a frequency premium for 6, 6.25 or 40.68 MHz without better exact-model outcomes.

## What the tiers mean

| Tier | My judgment | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **S — none** | Would require robust replicated current-model clinical evidence and durability | No reviewed home model qualifies |
| **A** | Strongest direct home clinical anchor with documented controls | Still mostly uncontrolled evidence |
| **A−** | My practical first shortlist for a current controlled-RF purchase | Does not establish superior clinical efficacy |
| **B** | Plausible alternative: human lineage, controlled combination evidence or credible regulatory bridge | Each type of support is stated separately |
| **C** | Interesting engineering/combination product; insufficient exact-model outcomes to prioritize for collagen alone | Not proof of ineffectiveness |
| **D** | I would not buy for collagen on the reviewed evidence and disclosure | Not a claim of zero effect |

Within-tier order is not a clinical league table. Price, prestige, watts and MHz do not earn efficacy points. Historical and study-only models are evidence references, not verified current purchases. A recommendation tier weighs probable usefulness and uncertainty; it cannot replace a head-to-head efficacy trial.

## Professional lineup: separate from home tiers

| Platform | My assessment | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Thermage CPT / FLX lineage** | Professional cooled-monopolar reference lane for tightening assessment | Exact generation matters; the 2026 randomized comparator used **CPT**, not FLX. Not a home-device upgrade. [[18]](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10103-026-04841-4) |
| **YOUMAGIC** | 2026 assessor-blinded randomized non-inferiority trial versus CPT; 230 enrolled / 212 analyzed, six months. Useful platform evidence. [[18]](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10103-026-04841-4) | Active control, retrospective registration and manufacturer-provided device support; not superiority or a collagen-growth assay |
| **Volnewmer, continuous-water-cooled monopolar RF** | 22-woman single-arm contour study with separate ex-vivo human and porcine collagen endpoints. Promising translational work. [[19]](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42352889/) | Animal collagen staining is not quantified human collagen gain or proof for a home handset |
| **RF microneedling: Morpheus8 / Potenza / Genius / related** | Indication-specific professional procedures for scars/texture; not a default stronger wand | FDA warns of serious complications and says not for home use. [[20]](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/safety-communications/potential-risks-certain-uses-radiofrequency-rf-microneedling-fda-safety-communication) |

For substantial laxity, acne scars or a larger intervention, seek a clinician assessment rather than chasing a home frequency/power upgrade. No valid conversion equates home sessions with one Thermage treatment.

## Specifications I would require

1. Exact model, local IFU, intended indication/population and valid authorization.
2. Load-specific **RF output**, waveform/duty cycle, electrode geometry, coupling requirements and timed area protocol. Charger watts do not qualify.
3. Temperature sensor location, numeric limit where disclosed, shutdown/restart, fault response and poor-contact behavior. Redundancy improves disclosure, not proven efficacy.
4. Outcomes using the sold hardware/regimen: comparator, withdrawals, blinded assessment, adverse events, sponsorship and maintenance follow-up.
5. Honest collagen endpoints: biopsy/staining, ultrasound thickness, spectral estimates, elasticity and wrinkle photography are different measurements.
6. Sustainable routine, local support, returns, warranty and total consumable cost.

**Around 1 MHz is a reasonable evidence anchor, not a biological optimum. Roughly 40–42°C contact-control limits are model-specific guardrails, not an adjustable collagen target.** See the [collagen specification synthesis](index.html#doc16).

## What would change these tiers

A current-model sham-controlled trial, valid durability follow-up, direct human collagen corroboration, unambiguous study-to-SKU matching or independent thermal maps could move a C/D product up. Unresolved hot spots, identity or support could move a shortlist candidate down. A discount improves value, not efficacy evidence.
