# TriPollar competitors: compare evidence and controls

*Updated 2026-10-01. The earlier temperature-aggressiveness ranking has been replaced because it mixed measured skin cutoffs, inferred dermal peaks and unsupported depth estimates.*

## What changed

TriPollar remains an established home RF alternative, but the available evidence does not establish it as the hottest validated consumer device, the closest consumer equivalent to Thermage, or the best clinical performer. A claimed 55°C dermal peak cannot be compared directly with a CurrentBody skin-control limit. Current variants require exact-model evidence rather than inheriting all original STOP study outcomes.

Start with the [expanded market/value census](index.html#doc12), [frequency and temperature research](index.html#doc13), and [searchable device comparison](rf_market_explorer.html).

## Competitors worth comparing

| Candidate | Why it matters | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CurrentBody ST030 | FDA technical file and redundant thermal feedback | No new subject-device outcome trial in clearance |
| NEWA / verified successor | Direct home De Novo clinical anchor | Original uncontrolled study; successor identity required |
| Sensilift Pro ST300 | FDA 1 MHz bipolar, 40±0.5°C skin maximum | Substantial equivalence without new clinical testing |
| Panasonic SR85 / SR86 / announced SR90 | 3 / 4 / 6 MHz RF and different features | No isolated-frequency superiority; SR90 prelaunch on October 1 |
| YA-MAN Bloom6 / regional ACE | 0.5–2.5 MHz; controlled regional-bundle study | Exact ACE-to-Bloom retail mapping unresolved |
| MimiSilk Vera | Gel-free 6.25 MHz stamping | Conflicting claimed dermal/surface temperatures; no exact-device trial recovered |
| EvenSkyn Lumo+ | High dermal-temperature and feedback claims | Independent thermal map not recovered; brand anatomy guarantees insufficient |
| AMIRO R1 / R3 routes | Claimed 42°C skin ceiling; six electrodes | Model redirect and exact clinical mapping unresolved |
| Silk'n Titan family | Older mixed RF/light human study | Current generation and RF-only contribution uncertain |
| Medicube Ultra Tune 40.68 | Lower-price RF/electrical-stimulation option | No clinical advantage established from high carrier number |

## Sources and conclusion

[CurrentBody FDA](source_docs/FDA_K232424_CurrentBody.pdf), [NEWA FDA](source_docs/FDA_DEN150005_NEWA.pdf), [Sensilift Pro FDA](source_docs/FDA_K250341_Sensilift_Pro.pdf), [TriPollar current protocols](https://uk.mytripollar.com/pages/how-to-use), [original clinical map](index.html#doc11), [current primary-source manifest](index.html#doc14).

Prefer exact-model thermal feedback, coupling/contact control, a usable IFU and measured clinical endpoints. The missing decisive evidence is a current-device randomized sham/head-to-head trial with common outcome scales and measured temperature-time histories. More heat, more watts and more MHz do not independently establish a better buy.
