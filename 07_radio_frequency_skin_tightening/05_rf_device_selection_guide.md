# RF Device Quick Selection Guide

*Updated October 2, 2026. Goal-first device research, not individual medical clearance. Read the [efficacy-informed tier list](index.html#doc10) and [collagen specification synthesis](index.html#doc16) for evidence and linked sources.*

## Choose by goal

| Goal | My next step |
|---|---|
| Mild facial wrinkles / subtle skin-quality improvement | Compare CurrentBody ST030 and original NEWA evidence; use the exact local IFU/population |
| Substantial laxity / larger lift | Professional evaluation; no home MHz or watt upgrade establishes clinic-equivalent outcomes |
| Acne scars / substantial texture change | Professional scar assessment; RF microneedling is a separate invasive procedure |
| Pigment / PIH / redness | Diagnosis, sunscreen and appropriate topical/vascular/pigment strategy first; RF is not the lead tool |
| A measured percentage of collagen growth | No reviewed home device can promise this; distinguish biopsy from thickness, elasticity and photographs |

## My home shortlist

**CurrentBody Skin RF ST030** is my practical first candidate: current U.S. listing and transparent thermal controls. **Original NEWA** is the strongest direct human clinical/regulatory anchor. Neither has proven head-to-head superiority. **Sensilift Pro ST300 and TriPollar STOP Vx2** are alternatives after identity, evidence transfer, local IFU and support checks.

CurrentBody/Sensilift filings are technical equivalence records without new subject-device trials. Original NEWA/STOP trials are largely uncontrolled. YA-MAN ACE/Jmoon controlled results are combination devices and do not automatically validate Bloom 6 or a current seller's unit. See the [clinical map](index.html#doc11).

## Before a purchase

Verify model/revision, local intended use, full IFU, actual RF-load output, numeric skin-temperature regulation where disclosed, contact/coupling, consumables, warranty and returns. Prices in old research pages are dated snapshots, not quotes. Registration or an unrelated predicate clearance does not validate all models in a brand.

Do not select by maximum MHz, total consumption watts, promised depth, or the hottest advertised temperature. Approximately 1 MHz is a documented home evidence anchor; it is not a universal collagen optimum. Home and professional protocols cannot be interchanged.

## Before use

Match the exact IFU population and area restrictions. CurrentBody's U.S. manual is limited to Fitzpatrick I–IV; original NEWA's pivotal file has a narrower female population. Implant, pregnancy, skin disease, heat-sensation, medication and recent-procedure exclusions differ by device. Ask the manufacturer/appropriate clinician when the manual conflicts with a website or your treatment history.

Use the specified gel/interface and timed routine; keep contact/motion as directed. Do not increase dwell time or disable a cutoff to chase collagen. Stop for persistent pain, burning, blistering, pigment or unexpected contour/nerve symptoms. The [preserved CurrentBody U.S. manual](source_docs/CurrentBody_RF_USA_IFU_2024.pdf) and [Vx2 manual](source_docs/TriPollar_STOP_VX2_manual.pdf) are evidence references; confirm the revision supplied with the purchased unit.

## What I would skip

I would not choose an unverified OEM facial RF device as a first buy, nor pay a premium for an unvalidated high-frequency/high-heat collagen claim. MimiSilk and unresolved EvenSkyn/AMIRO generations remain lower buying-confidence entries, not proven ineffective devices. RF microneedling is professional-only; FDA explicitly says it should not be used at home.
