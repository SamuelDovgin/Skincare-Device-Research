How estimates and evidence classes work

Battery energy = nominal V × mAh / 1000 (Wh). Nominal total average = Wh ÷ stated runtime in hours (W). This assumes the nominal battery energy and the stated runtime belong to the same unit and use condition. Usable energy, intensity and the fraction used for RF are unknown. It is not a measured draw or RF efficiency calculation.

“Documented” includes numerical FDA specifications and current manufacturer manuals. Historical manual conflicts and marketing/seller values appear in “all RF maxima.” A numeric bench result means the document discloses a number, rather than only saying a test passed. Wattage values at different loads are not normalized here.

117 records include modes, probes, aliases and historical revisions. The prior 51-record census is carried forward with its older check date and unknown RF output. This is a broad research inventory, not an exhaustive market or patent/FDA audit.
