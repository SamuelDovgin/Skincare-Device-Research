# Thermage versus home RF: what is actually comparable?

*Corrected October 2, 2026. The earlier table and diagrams mixed manufacturer claims, estimates, thermal sensor limits and clinical outcomes. Unsupported fixed depths, universal dermal temperature bands, improvement percentages and session conversions have been removed.*

**For a home device mimicking the Thermage goal, start with the [closest home-device guide](index.html#doc18) and [home feature comparison](rf_spec_fit_visualizer.html#home-thermage).** My practical first shortlist is CurrentBody ST030; original wired NEWA has stronger direct clinical evidence with conditional availability.

**For professional alternatives, start with the [Thermage equivalents and professional service shortlist](index.html#doc17).** Volnewmer has a small direct FLX comparison; YOUMAGIC has a larger CPT comparison. XERF, Oligio and Density have promising human evidence with weaker substitution support. No reviewed home device demonstrates Thermage-equivalent results.

## The meaningful comparison

| Decision | Professional Thermage / alternatives | Home RF |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Clinician-operated cooled RF, model-specific tips and protocol; monopolar platforms use a return electrode | Usually bipolar/multipolar handheld delivery with model-specific interface and motion |
| Human evidence | Some exact-platform active comparisons; distinguish Thermage CPT from FLX | Mostly small/uncontrolled or historical studies; some current models rely on regulatory bridges |
| Thermal controls | Cooling/contact/impedance and protocol differ across platforms | Original NEWA and CurrentBody disclose numeric skin-control limits; these are not depth-resolved dermal measurements |
| Outcome expectation | Assess wrinkles/firmness/laxity with the clinician; results vary and a substantial lift is not guaranteed | Modest wrinkle/skin-quality goals within the purchased model's IFU |
| Collagen claim | Appearance, thickness and elasticity are not direct collagen quantification | No universal collagen-growth percentage can be assigned to the lineup |
| Equivalence | Requires a properly designed human comparison for the same generation/goal | Matching frequency or accumulating minutes does not establish equivalence |

## What does not identify the closest device

**Higher MHz:** carrier frequency alone does not determine field depth or outcome. A 6.25 MHz home product does not become a Thermage substitute because its carrier resembles 6.78 MHz.

**Higher advertised temperature:** a contact thermistor cutoff, a manufacturer dermal claim and an ex-vivo depth trace measure different things. Do not compare them as a common dose scale or chase a universal collagen threshold.

**Larger W or session energy:** nominal platform maxima, mains consumption and absorbed tissue power are distinct. Summed joules cannot equate different tip geometry, pulse timing, cooling and anatomical distribution.

**A fixed depth diagram:** RF heating is a spatial temperature history influenced by geometry, tissue impedance and cooling. Half electrode spacing or a stated millimeter value cannot prove a uniform treatment column or a specific SMAS effect.

**Regulatory predicate:** substantial equivalence for specified indications does not establish equal cosmetic effect or transfer every predicate indication. Newer professional platforms may have narrower U.S. indication wording than Thermage FLX.

## My current choices

For a credible alternative professional service, first investigate **Volnewmer for the direct FLX comparison** or **YOUMAGIC for the larger CPT comparison**, subject to exact-model/local authorization and an experienced provider. For mild home wrinkle goals, **CurrentBody ST030 is the practical first shortlist** and **original NEWA the direct clinical evidence anchor**. These are separate decisions, with no home-to-clinic conversion.
