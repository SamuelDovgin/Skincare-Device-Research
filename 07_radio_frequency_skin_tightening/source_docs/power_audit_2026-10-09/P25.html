How to Use MimiSilk Vera RF Sculpt: Complete Guide & Expected Results

July 19, 2026
Share

Vera RF Sculpt is a gel-free, 6.25MHz multipolar RF device built to visibly lift and firm the skin without the mess of traditional conductive gels. Getting the best results depends on using the correct stamp technique, choosing the right intensity level, and understanding what's actually happening beneath the surface of your skin. This guide walks through exactly how to use Vera, area by area, along with an expected results timeline.

What Vera Is Designed to Do

Vera uses displacement current to send 6.25MHz multipolar RF energy directly into the dermis, bypassing the epidermis without needing a conductive gel (read more about the gel-free technology here). Unlike gliding RF devices, Vera is designed to be used with a controlled stamping motion — placed onto the skin, held briefly, then lifted and moved to the next spot. This device is built to support firmer-looking skin and more defined facial contours over time, rather than deliver a surgical-level lift.

Before You Start

Cleanse your skin first, then apply a light layer of facial oil or moisturizer. This step helps hydrate the skin and supports comfortable, even contact between the device and your skin during treatment.

From there, select your intensity level based on your skin's sensitivity and experience with RF devices. Vera offers three power levels, each calibrated to reach a specific dermal temperature:

Why Your Skin May Not Feel Very Warm

Here's something important to understand about how Vera works: you may not feel intense heat on the surface of your skin, but that doesn't mean the device isn't working. Vera's RF energy is designed to bypass the epidermis (the surface layer you can feel) and concentrate its heating effect in the dermis, the deeper layer where collagen lives. So even if your skin feels only mildly warm to the touch, the dermis temperature underneath is actually rising.

This is intentional — RF technology works by heating the deeper dermal layer to trigger collagen remodeling, while leaving the surface layer relatively undisturbed. Don't judge whether Vera is "working" based on how hot your skin feels on the outside; the real action is happening beneath what you can sense.

Choose Your Intensity and Frequency

  • Level 1: Can be used daily on the full face and neck. If you're new to Vera, stay at Level 1 for your first month to let your skin adjust.
  • Levels 2–3: Start with full face and neck sessions every other day. Once your skin comfortably tolerates treatment, you can move to daily use if you want.
  • At any level, scale back frequency if your skin feels sensitive, irritated, or uncomfortable. Consistency at a comfortable level matters more than pushing to the highest setting too quickly.

Intelligent Time Control

Vera doesn't require you to manually time each stamp. The device uses real-time monitoring of skin impedance to dynamically adjust energy output, delivering 6–10 seconds of treatment per stamp, with a full-face session taking less than 10 minutes. This built-in adjustment helps ensure the device provides safe, effective energy suited to different skin types and different treatment areas.

How to Use Vera: The Stamp Technique

  1. Cleanse your skin and apply a light layer of facial oil or moisturizer.
  2. Turn on Vera and select your intensity level.
  3. Place the treatment head flat against your skin and hold for one stamp (roughly 6–10 seconds, guided automatically by the device).
  4. Lift the device, move to the next adjacent spot, and repeat in an organized, methodical pattern.
  5. Avoid sliding Vera continuously across your skin — this device is designed for stamping, not gliding.
  6. Don't repeatedly stamp the same small spot chasing faster results; move steadily across the treatment area instead.
  7. Follow up with your mild, hydrating skincare to cool and soothe the skin. You may apply the PDRN face mask included with Vera.

Area-Specific Techniques

Jawline

Follow the side of the jawline with an upward stamping motion, working from beneath the jaw contour upward toward the jawbone.

Loose or Sagging Skin

For areas with visible laxity, use your free hand to gently smooth and lift the skin first, then stamp Vera onto the now-flatter, supported area. This helps the device make more even contact and can improve results.

Eye Contour

Gently lift the skin upward toward the orbital bone with your free hand before treating. Treat only along the orbital bone — the firm, bony ridge surrounding your eye socket. Never aim Vera at the eyeball or place it directly over the eye itself. This is the same approach used with the Iris laser around the eyes.

Small or Curved Areas

The full treatment head doesn't need to sit completely tight against your skin. Vera's treatment head has 4 electrode pads, but the device can operate normally as long as 2 of the 4 electrode pads are making contact with your skin. This makes it easier to treat smaller facial contours and curved areas without needing perfectly flat positioning.

Areas to Avoid

Do not use Vera on or near:

  • The eyeballs — treat only along the orbital bone, never directly over the eye
  • The thyroid area, at the lower front of the neck
  • The heart and chest area
  • Private or intimate areas
  • Broken, irritated, inflamed, or otherwise compromised skin

What Vera Should Feel Like

Expect a comfortable warming sensation during treatment — remember, the more significant heating is happening in your dermis, not necessarily something you'll feel intensely at the surface.

Treatment should never feel sharply painful, burning, or intensely uncomfortable. If irritation persists after a session, reduce your intensity level or frequency, and allow your skin more time to adjust before progressing further.

Expected Results

Immediately After Treatment

Skin may feel comfortably warm, and some people notice a temporarily firmer-looking appearance right after a session, as the immediate thermal effect takes hold.

7–14 Days

Early visible lifting or tightening effects may begin to appear with consistent use, as the initial collagen response develops.

Around 28 Days

Collagen-renewal benefits tend to become more noticeable around this point, including smoother-looking skin texture and more visible improvement in jawline and facial contour definition.

With Continued, Consistent Use

Results are gradual and vary based on your baseline skin laxity, consistency of use, and the intensity level chosen. Give the process time — collagen remodeling isn't an overnight change, even though the dermis is heating up with every session.

How to Track Your Progress

Take baseline photos before you start using Vera. Recreate the same lighting, angle, distance, and facial expression every 2–4 weeks so you can compare fairly. Watch for gradual changes in jawline definition, neck smoothness, or overall skin firmness rather than expecting a dramatic change after only one or two sessions.

FAQs

Do I need gel to use MimiSilk Vera?

No. Vera uses gel-free displacement current technology, which allows RF energy to pass through the epidermis to the dermis without a conductive gel. Just cleanse your skin and apply a light facial oil or moisturizer before use.

Why doesn't my skin feel very warm during treatment?

Vera is designed to heat the dermis, the deeper skin layer, rather than the surface epidermis you can feel. Even if your skin only feels mildly warm to the touch, your dermis temperature is actually rising to between 45°C and 52°C depending on your selected level.

How often should I use Vera on my full face and neck?

Level 1 can be used daily, and beginners should stay at this level for their first month. Levels 2 and 3 should start at every-other-day use, moving to daily only once your skin comfortably tolerates it.

How long does a full session take?

Each stamp takes about 6–10 seconds, with a full-face treatment session taking roughly 10 minutes total, thanks to Vera's intelligent time control that adjusts energy output automatically.

Can I use Vera around my eyes?

Yes, but only along the orbital bone — the bony ridge surrounding your eye socket. Never point the device at your eyeball or treat directly over the eye itself.

Do all 4 electrode pads need to touch my skin?

No. Vera's treatment head has four electrode pads, but the device will operate normally as long as at least 2 of them are making contact with your skin, which is especially useful for smaller or curved areas.

When should I expect results from Vera?

Some people notice a temporary firming effect immediately after a session. Early lifting or tightening effects can appear around 7–14 days, with more noticeable collagen-renewal results typically emerging around the 28-day mark.

What areas should I avoid when using Vera?

Avoid the eyeballs directly, the thyroid area, the heart and chest area, and private or intimate areas. Also avoid broken, irritated, or inflamed skin.

Can Vera cause in fat loss?

The 6.25 MHz frequency is close to that of Thermage (6.78 MHz); however, due to the safety restrictions of household device, its power output is only one-quarter that of professional-grade RF devices found in clinics. It also lacks the penetration depth to reach the subcutaneous fat or muscle layers. This distinction in penetration depth means that it falls far short of achieving any lipolytic (fat-dissolving) effects.
