    How to Use

    Fully charge before first use (3–5 hours).

    Apply a conductive gel or water-based serum to clean skin.

    Power on and select your preferred intensity level. If you’re new to RF devices, start on the Low setting.

    Glide the device gently across the face and neck using slow, upward motions.

    Focus on areas such as the forehead, crow’s feet, jawline, and around the mouth.

    The device automatically shuts off after 15 minutes.

    For best results, use 3–5 times per week as part of your skincare routine.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is RF therapy safe for home use?

    Yes. The device uses CE-certified Radio Frequency technology designed for at-home use. Begin with the Low setting and gradually increase as your skin adjusts. Avoid use if you are pregnant, have metal implants, or have active skin conditions in the treatment area.

    Do I need a gel or serum?

    Yes. Always apply a conductive gel or water-based serum before use. This helps improve conductivity and provides a more comfortable treatment experience. Never use the device on completely dry skin.

    How soon will I see results?

    Many users notice their skin feels smoother and more refreshed after the first few treatments. More visible improvements typically develop with consistent use over several weeks.

    Can I replace the electrode head?

    Yes. The platinum electrode head is replaceable, making it easy to maintain hygiene and long-term performance.
