Is it suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. Our formulas are thoughtfully developed with gentle, skin-friendly ingredients that are suitable for most skin types, including sensitive skin. If you have highly reactive skin or known allergies, we recommend performing a patch test before first use.

How do I build a routine?

Start with the basics: cleanse, moisturize, and apply sunscreen in the morning; cleanse and apply a nourishing treatment at night. Once your skin adjusts, you can layer in a serum or targeted treatment based on your specific needs. Check out our Routine Guide for more detailed advice based on your skin type.

Are the ingredients natural?

Yes. Our products prioritize naturally derived ingredients, carefully selected for both safety and effectiveness. Some formulas include science-backed actives to boost results, but always at gentle, skin-safe concentrations.

Can I mix products together?

Yes. Our products are designed to layer well together. That said, if you're using stronger actives like retinol or AHA/BHA, we recommend spacing out application times to avoid irritation.

Are your products cruelty-free?

Yes. None of our products are tested on animals at any stage of development. We're committed to an ethical, environmentally conscious production process.
