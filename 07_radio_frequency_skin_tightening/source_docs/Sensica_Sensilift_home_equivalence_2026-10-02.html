Fine lines and lost firmness don't start on the surface - they start beneath it. Sensilift™ works where they begin. This clinically tested, FDA-cleared anti-aging device is powered by Sensica's own Dynamic RF™ technology, delivering gentle, controlled heat into the deeper layers of your skin and adapting to your skin's condition in real time to stimulate its natural collagen production.

What Sensilift™ works on:

  • Softens the look of fine lines and wrinkles on the forehead, cheeks, and around the eyes
  • Restores firmness and lift to the jawline, cheeks, and neck
  • Smooths texture for a visibly healthier, more radiant glow
  • Rebuilds plumper, more youthful-looking contours as collagen renews over time

Use it across the forehead, cheeks, jawline, and neck, and gently around the eyes, staying at least 2/5 inch (1 cm) from the lash line.
