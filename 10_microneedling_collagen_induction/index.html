A practical research lane for microneedling: where the evidence is strongest, what FDA has actually authorized, why consumer rollers do not equal SkinPen/Exceed treatments, and where RF microneedling crosses into professional-only procedure risk.

Research read: microneedling is most credible for professional treatment of atrophic acne scars. Superficial home tools may be cosmetic maintenance, but FDA has not authorized OTC medical microneedling devices, and RF microneedling is explicitly not a home-use category.
