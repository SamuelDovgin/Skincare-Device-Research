What it can help

Very shallow needling can fit cosmetic texture or glow narratives, but it should not be treated as acne-scar collagen remodeling.

Practical read: the more the claim moves from surface feel to scar/wrinkle remodeling, the more it depends on a cleared device, trained setting, site-specific protocol, and adverse-event controls.
