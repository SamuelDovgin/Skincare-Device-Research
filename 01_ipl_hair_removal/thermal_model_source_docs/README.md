| [`PMC5718238_broadband_IPL_pulse_structure_model_fulltext.xml`](PMC5718238_broadband_IPL_pulse_structure_model_fulltext.xml) | Ash et al., *Journal of Applied Clinical Medical Physics* 2012; [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5718238/); full-text XML retrieved from Europe PMC 2026-07-10 | Primary IPL-specific Monte Carlo plus heat-diffusion model calibrated against skin thermography. Defines TRT as the time to lose 50% of induced heat; uses about 40–100 ms for 200–300 µm follicles; compares a 1 ms close stack with a 15 ms spaced stack and finds more off-time loses more target heat. Supports correcting the simulator's half-time math and separating close versus spaced pulse trains. |
| [`PMC10732256_skin_tone_melanosome_optical_model_fulltext.xml`](PMC10732256_skin_tone_melanosome_optical_model_fulltext.xml) | Else et al., *Journal of Biomedical Optics* 2024; [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10732256/); full-text XML retrieved from Europe PMC 2026-07-10 | Primary computational optical study validated against diffuse-reflectance/ITA ranges. Uses six logarithmically spaced epidermal melanosome fractions from 2% to 40% and assigns them to approximate Fitzpatrick I–VI. Supports the simulator's continuous skin-melanin curve and half-step interpolation. It is a photoacoustic optical model, not an IPL hair-temperature validation or an individual melanin measurement. |
| [`Kato_2002_histological_changes_hair_removal_lasers.pdf`](Kato_2002_histological_changes_hair_removal_lasers.pdf) | Kato et al., *Journal of Nippon Medical School* 2002; [J-STAGE article](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jnms/69/6/69_6_564/_article/-char/en); official open-access PDF retrieved 2026-07-10 | Primary human histology after ruby/alexandrite hair removal. Supports immediate follicular damage plus one-month cystic and foreign-body changes; does not establish a universal temperature threshold. |

## Linked primary papers not mirrored

- Ataie-Fashtami et al. 2011, DOI [10.1089/pho.2010.2895](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1089/pho.2010.2895): hair-removal-specific LITCIT model and the Arrhenius parameter pair now used in the simulator. Publisher full text is restricted, so this project links the official record rather than preserving an unauthorized copy.
- Fiskerstrand et al. 2003, [PubMed 12766964](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12766964/): clinical comparison plus heat-diffusion model using a 65 °C damage convention. Publisher full text is restricted.
- Topping et al. 2000, [PubMed 10884072](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10884072/): ex vivo human-follicle thermal imaging and histology. Publisher full text is restricted.
- McCoy et al. 1999, [PubMed 10100652](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10100652/): histology after one 3 ms ruby-laser treatment; no evidence of permanent follicle death after one treatment. Publisher full text is restricted.

## Scope note

This corpus anchors the **damage-model and pulse-carryover corrections**. The simulator now distinguishes (1) residual temperature governed by target cooling time and (2) a pulse-history sensitivity observed by Viera-Mármol et al. even when ≥2 s spacing allowed measured hair temperature to return to ambient. That sensitivity layer remains a cross-wavelength extrapolation for broadband home IPL. Device-specific FDA filings and manuals remain in the parent IPL folder and `fda_data_pipeline/`; professional SHR outcome studies remain linked from doc 16 and the simulator source list.
