DPL Technology

DPL (Dye Pulsed Light) represents an advanced, high-end evolution of IPL (Intense Pulsed Light), distinguished by its utilization of precise narrow-spectrum light technology. This system is engineered to emit an intense pulsed light confined within a narrow band of approximately 100nm. This specific spectral choice is paramount, as the 100nm band simultaneously captures the optimal absorption peaks for both melanin and oxyhemoglobin. Consequently, DPL ensures that the treatment energy is concentrated with high accuracy. The core action, facilitated by selective photothermolysis, allows the system to efficiently target and resolve underlying dermatological issues, specifically deep-layer melanin deposition and capillary dilation.

How JOVS's DPL Technology Work?

JOVS has successfully adapted this high-end technology for compact home-use devices through dedicated research and proprietary innovation. By thoroughly studying the effects of spectral intervals and energy density on the skin, alongside the absorption curve of carnosine under intense pulsed light, JOVS developed a unique proprietary filtering algorithm. This algorithm precisely filters out harmful ultraviolet and ineffective light, purifying the pulse to generate precise narrow-spectrum outputs of 500 - 650nm(Blacken PRO) and 700 - 800nm (Blacken X). Upon absorption by the skin, these specific wavelengths act selectively on the chromophores (melanin and hemoglobin): light energy converts into heat, selectively heating the target tissue while sparing the surrounding normal skin. Furthermore, the system’s rapid pulsed-light release and optimized energy density induce controlled effects, including thermal coagulation, fragmentation, or denaturation. This collective process stimulates skin metabolism, promotes the regeneration of collagen, and facilitates the closure of targeted blood vessels, ultimately leading to the improvement of multiple skin concerns with proven safety and efficacy.

JOVS's DPL Technology Targets Specific Skin Concerns

JOVS DPL technology, primarily utilizing the 500 - 650nm narrow band, achieves multidimensional skin improvement by strategically targeting melanin and hemoglobin. 
For pigmentation and acne marks, such as spots, sunspots, and freckles, light energy is selectively absorbed by melanin in WHITEN mode, where the heat causes thermal coagulation and fragmentation of the pigment clusters, which are subsequently cleared through accelerated metabolism. 
For redness and broken capillaries, including rosacea and vascular concerns, the energy is selectively absorbed by hemoglobin in dilated capillaries in REDNESS mode. The resulting photothermal effect induces coagulative changes, leading to the closure, shrinkage, and absorption of these vessels, thereby effectively mitigating vascular dilation and diffuse redness. 
Brightening, radiance, and foundational anti-aging by simultaneously dispersing uneven surface pigmentation and crucially promoting the renewal and reorganization of dermal collagen and elastin fibers. This restorative action enhances underlying elasticity, fundamentally improving dullness and uneven tone. Finally, by stimulating dermal collagen regeneration and accelerating metabolism through combined photothermal and photochemical effects, DPL increases underlying skin density, achieving noticeable refinement of enlarged and congested pores.
