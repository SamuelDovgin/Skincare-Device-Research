AY101 verification gate

PAKISS is the best Alibaba counterparty found, but it is not a verified JOVS-equivalent optical device. Require exact-model evidence for:

  1. Spectrometer trace from 400–1,200 nm by mode.
  2. Fluence in J/cm² at every level.
  3. Pulse duration, count, delay, and repetition rate.
  4. Active spot dimensions and stabilized cooling temperature.
  5. AY101-specific photobiological, EMC, and safety reports.

No items 1–3 means no optical-equivalence claim.

Evidence boundary and source assumptions

Prices, availability, and supplier metrics were observed on 2026-07-09 and can change. JOVS specifications are official brand claims; Alibaba specifications are seller claims. No independent device-specific clinical trial was located for Blacken, Blacken Pro, Blacken X, or AY101.

The professional ranges are context, not home-device dosing targets: 65-patient DPL rosacea study (8.4–10.6 J/cm²) and 181-patient sensitive-skin cohort (5.0–6.8 J/cm²). FDA K231800 is a hair-removal clearance and does not verify Blacken photofacial claims.
