Maximum useful coverage—not maximum irritation

This category separates acquired body hyperpigmentation from natural baseline tone and medical causes, ranks classic and newer actives, builds a phased routine for years-long maintenance, and adds a dated buy-now product/value tier list without treating melanocyte injury as a desirable endpoint.

Fast answer: start with trigger control + niacinamide 4% or topical TXA + azelaic acid. Add one direct inhibitor (Thiamidol, Melasyl, or 4-n-butylresorcinol), then one weekly turnover lane only if the skin remains calm.
Hard boundary: monobenzone, mercury/unlabeled bleaches, chronic steroid mixtures, injectable glutathione, and deliberate melanocyte destruction are not maximal skincare. They are unsafe, unapproved, or medically inappropriate pathways.
