Open text pathway and candidate map
Stimulus (UVB/UVA/visible light, friction, shaving, inflammation) → keratinocyte signals (p53/POMC/α-MSH and PGE2/ET-1/SCF) → MC1R/cAMP/PKA/CREB → MITF → TYR: tyrosine → L-DOPA → dopaquinone → TYRP1/DCT and eumelanin or cysteine-linked pheomelanin → melanosome maturation and PAR-2-related transfer → pigmented keratinocytes / visible epidermal pigment → dermal melanophages and persistence. Evidence-mapped candidates: stimulus: sunscreen/trigger control [controlled human prevention] plasmin/inflammation: topical TXA [controlled human; body translation limited] transfer: niacinamide [controlled axillary RCT + mechanism], NAG [limited formula RCT] inflammation/TYR: azelaic acid [controlled facial trials] TYR: Thiamidol [controlled human + mechanism], HQ/TCC [controlled but finite], kojic/arbutin/resorcinols [limited human], KT-939 [emerging] precursor/redox: Melasyl [limited controlled facial formula], cysteamine [controlled facial evidence], vitamin C/glutathione/resveratrol [limited or emerging] turnover: retinoids and glycolic/lactic acid [controlled/limited reviews and texture studies] barrier: ceramides/urea/petrolatum [supportive recovery lane] toxic history: mercury and monobenzone [historical/regulatory only].
