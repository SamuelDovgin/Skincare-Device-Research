Your most frequently asked questions about Sensitive Areas

Pigmentbio Sensitive areas is intended to illuminate and unify the skin in areas where friction, hair removal or contact with clothing causes discoloration and the gradual appearance of darker areas (knees, neck, underarms, thighs, groin).

Pigmentbio Sensitive areas effectively reduces the appearance of dark spots while presenting a very good tolerance, which allows a use on the most sensitive zones.

Skin marks and spots reflect our daily lives: pollution, excessive sun exposure, age, pregnancy, irritations, etc. These hyperpigmentation disorders are due to an unusual production of melanin.

Erasing these spots and regaining a radiant and unified complexion requires a lot of effort and time, while women want immediate results.

Skin can be discolored due to several factors: friction (with clothing, for example), hair removal and skin reactivity.
