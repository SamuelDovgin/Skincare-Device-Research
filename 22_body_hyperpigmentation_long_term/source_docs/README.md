## Evidence boundary

These files do not establish that the proposed full routine was tested as one combination. Most efficacy evidence is facial and short-term; the axillary literature is small; and proprietary-active research is often manufacturer-involved. Enzyme assays support mechanism, not comparative consumer-product outcomes.
