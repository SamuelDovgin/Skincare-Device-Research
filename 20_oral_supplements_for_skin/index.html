What oral supplements can actually change

A source-backed map of oral nutrients, collagen peptides, HA, ceramides, carotenoids, botanicals, probiotics, and disease-specific supplements studied for skin endpoints.

Fast answer: collagen/HA are the main hydration-and-elasticity experiments; carotenoids/Polypodium are UV adjuncts; zinc, vitamin D, and probiotics are condition-specific; vitamin C is essential nutrition but not a proven oral wrinkle megadose.

Recommended reading order

  1. Define one metric and one time window.
  2. Read the evidence map and inspect funding/endpoint caveats.
  3. Audit all overlapping nutrient amounts and upper-limit assumptions before adding a product.
  4. Pick one lane for 8–12 weeks; do not start a stack.
  5. Reassess whether the change is worth continuing.
