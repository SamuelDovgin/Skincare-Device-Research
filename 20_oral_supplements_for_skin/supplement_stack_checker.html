Stack and upper-limit checker

Add amounts per serving and actual daily servings across products. Nothing is uploaded or saved after the page closes.

Safety and uncertainty statement: this is adult arithmetic, not a “safe dose” calculator. Below a UL does not mean appropriate; above a general UL does not tell a clinician how to manage a medical protocol. The tool omits disease, pregnancy, interactions, allergens, product assay, collagen/HA/botanicals, and nutrients without a UL. Read the model and sources.

Optional known baseline

For vitamin A, C, D, zinc, and selenium, enter known food/fortified/medication intake if available. For niacin and vitamin E, enter only supplemental/fortified baseline. Blank means the calculated result is a minimum known total.
