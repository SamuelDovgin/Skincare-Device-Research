Category 15 · injectable biostimulation

Sculptra is gradual structural remodeling—not a reversible spot filler or a facelift.

The strongest on-label evidence supports cheek wrinkles and facial contour deficiencies in properly selected immunocompetent adults. The key trade is gradual, distributed improvement and potential durability versus less reversibility, multiple sessions, delayed nodules, and rare but severe injection complications.
