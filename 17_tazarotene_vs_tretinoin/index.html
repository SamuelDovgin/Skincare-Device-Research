Two prescription retinoids, two different evidence stories

Tazarotene has a strong direct acne signal against low-strength tretinoin and credible photoaging trials. Tretinoin has the deeper, more established human evidence for photoaging and collagen-related remodeling.

Read the percentage carefully: 0.1% tazarotene and 0.1% tretinoin are not a validated potency match. Compare molecule, vehicle, endpoint, study duration, irritation, and indication—not the number alone.
