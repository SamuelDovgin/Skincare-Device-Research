Which peptides actually deserve attention?

The short answer: palmitoyl pentapeptide-4 has the best direct topical fine-line trial, acetyl hexapeptide-8 has the clearest expression-line signal, and copper tripeptide-1 is promising but much less clinically decisive than its hype. Peptides are gentle adjuncts—not substitutes for sunscreen, retinoids, Botox, or target-matched devices.

Evidence boundary: the 2026 systematic review found 19 randomized trials but only two high-quality topical studies. Most pooled benefit came from oral formulations, so this archive does not convert mechanistic peptide claims into guaranteed visible results.
