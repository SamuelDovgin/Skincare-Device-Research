How to use the selected anchor

  • The displayed number is one selected public formulation-cost anchor, generally the largest visible pack that plausibly resembles creator purchasing.
  • For a branded technology, the same branded premix or solution wins over a pure peptide with the same INCI.
  • A Formulate Labs suspension can be used as a potency-matched replacement benchmark, but it is not evidence that a retail brand buys from Formulate Labs.
  • A quote gap is preferable to a false number. It means the correct material was located but its unit price was not public.
