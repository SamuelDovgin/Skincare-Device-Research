## Identity and optical caveats

- The PL300 name, dimensions, LED count, weight, and wavelength set closely match current and historical Shenzhen SAIDI PL-series listings. That makes SAIDI the **probable** manufacturer/listing family, but the screenshots do not visibly name the seller.
- Neither these screenshots nor the SAIDI pages checked on 2026-07-13 state a 60° beam angle for this exact PL300. The 60° calculation in the research page is therefore a user-recalled optical scenario, not a verified specification.
- The irradiance graphic does not identify the meter, measurement protocol, panel mode, warm-up time, grid location, or whether its figures are center-point or area-average values.
- Current indexed listings show 400–420 W variants while these screenshots show 520–540 W. This is meaningful variant/spec drift; company-family documents should be used loosely, not as a serial-number-specific manual.
