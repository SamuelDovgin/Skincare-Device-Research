Study-dot interpretation and protocol bookmarks

Does a condition-specific positive signal matter?

Yes—for that condition and endpoint. A sham-controlled fibromyalgia result is more informative for people with fibromyalgia than a null sports study, and it supports further replication. It does not establish a general-health benefit, prove that the same dose helps healthy adults, or locate the peak of the orange curve. Population, outcome, geometry, and dose all have to match before a result can be generalized.

    Power sanity check and source reports

    Power sanity check: 360 W across 300 dual-chip packages is about 1.2 W/package, so it is plausible. Comparable mixed red/NIR lab data convert roughly 25–36% of wall input into measured radiant output, mapping 360 W to approximately 90–130 W optical. The 50:50 allocation assumption does not change total watts. The device.report family constrains point irradiance more strongly than the optical-total estimate: lower per-package drive lowers the expected grid average, while tighter optics could still produce a higher center spot. Point irradiance is not whole-body average irradiance.

    How to use this planner and interpret the schedule

    Evidence-weighted starting plan: anchor the exposure at 10 J/cm² per side per session and keep the schedule separate at 2 nonconsecutive treatment days/week for a 4–6 week tracked trial. The resulting 20 J/cm²/week is derived bookkeeping, not a clinically validated optimum.

    Skin-goal presets: anti-aging/wrinkles uses 5 J/cm² to the face three times weekly; the pigmentation and redness settings are lower-confidence, red-only tolerance tests. They are one facial pass—not front plus back—and use 24 inches to improve uniformity and reduce heat. The selected-mode timer is still an estimate until the panel is measured in that mode.

    Why weekly dose is secondary: equal weekly totals do not prove equal biology. For example, 10 J/cm² twice weekly and 4 J/cm² five times weekly both total 20 J/cm²/week, but no whole-body trial establishes that they are interchangeable. The experimental five-day and twice-daily presets are calculations, not recommendations.

    Use a measured body-position irradiance, not LED wattage or a seller headline: time (seconds) = 1000 × target J/cm² ÷ mW/cm². Heat, eye exposure, medications, pregnancy, active cancer, photosensitivity, and medical conditions warrant clinician/device-IFU review before self-experimentation.
