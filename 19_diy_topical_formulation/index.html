This topic answers a practical question: which online powders are genuinely reasonable DIY candidates once efficacy, pH, solubility, oxidation, preservation, and storage are all counted together? It now also covers a measured hypochlorous-water workflow for a cautious pillowcase experiment and a separate decision path for future facial use.

Fast answer: L-ascorbic acid is the clearest fresh-batch candidate; glycolic acid is a credible active but a poor first powder-in-water project; HA is useful but usually better bought finished; water-based multi-use batches are the major contamination trap; DIY HOCl belongs in a measured fabric-experiment lane, not a facial-spray shortcut.
