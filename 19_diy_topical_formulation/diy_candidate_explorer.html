Evidence boundary

  • Vitamin C protocol — L-ascorbic-acid pH, oxidation, and storage context.
  • Glycolic-acid evidence — human 5% trial, pH/vehicle, and AHA safety context.
  • FDA microbiological safety — contamination routes and storage cautions.
  • HA randomized trial — finished-lotion hydration evidence, not a raw-powder recipe.
