Bottom line: no at-home IPL device — branded or OEM — is FDA-cleared for anything but hair removal. For the hair goal the best OEM target is the Fansizhe T023A; the lowest-risk branded pick is the Nood Flasher 2.0. For pigment/redness, lead with tinted SPF + topicals + LED — not IPL. DermRays, RF, microneedling, HIFU, vitamin C, zinc oxide barrier science, and topical peptides now have their own lanes so unlike mechanisms and evidence are not conflated.
