This is the archive’s cross-industry layer: a visual map and evidence synthesis across devices, ingredients, peptides, injectables, and periprocedural care.

Core rule: the larger a node, the more mapped human evidence and reporting support it has. That is not a promise that every product in the category works, and it is not a ranking by market size or social popularity.
