# Goal-to-intervention matrix

*Compiled 2026-08-02. “First-line” here means the most defensible starting lane for the stated goal, not a personalized medical order.*

## 0. Bottom line

Use the lowest-complexity option that matches the endpoint, give it enough time to show an effect, and add escalation only when the target, risk, and measurement plan are clear. More devices are not automatically a better regimen.

## 1. Practical matrix

| Goal | Start with | Consider next | Be cautious / skip as a first move | Measurement that matters |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Melasma / PIH / uneven pigment | Broad-spectrum sunscreen; for visible-light-sensitive pigment, a well-formulated tinted product; a clinician-guided topical plan | Selected low-fluence pigment or fractional protocols with skin-type-aware follow-up | Home IPL sold for hair removal; aggressive stacking of acids, heat, and needles | Standardized photos, mMASI or validated pigment index, relapse at 3–6 months |
| Fine lines / photoaging | Photoprotection + retinoid-compatible routine | Fractional laser, microneedling, red LED/PBM, selected RF | Unnamed “collagen” devices with no output or endpoint data | Wrinkle scale, texture, patient-reported benefit, ≥3–6 month follow-up |
| Atrophic acne scars | Treat active acne first; then choose a scar-specific procedure | Microneedling, RF microneedling, fractional CO2/Er:Glass/other fractional laser based on scar and skin type | Home dermarollers and consumer lasers with unknown sterile/depth controls | ECCA/Goodman scale, standardized photos, PIH/downtime |
| Acne | Proven topical or prescription regimen plus tolerable moisturizer | Home LED adjunct, clinician-selected light/laser for refractory disease | Probiotic/prebiotic marketing as a replacement for acne therapy | Lesion counts, severity scale, irritation/adherence, relapse |
| Mild laxity / firmness | Set anatomy and expectation: topical skin quality is not a lift | RF or MFU/HIFU with verified device/indication; injectables when volume is the issue | Comparing home RF heat with a clinic focal-depth protocol; DIY RF | Standardized laxity scale, contour photos, pain/downtime, 3–6 months |
| Hair removal | Selective laser/IPL matched to hair color, skin tone, and body site | Home IPL or diode for appropriate candidates and maintenance | Using an IPL hair device for pigment/rejuvenation; treating white/blonde/red hair as if it were dark hair | Hair counts and regrowth at months 3, 6, and 12; paradoxical hypertrichosis |
| Barrier irritation / recovery | Gentle cleansing, moisturizer/lipids, photoprotection, stop irritants | Zinc oxide/protectant or procedure-specific dressing when indicated | Heat, needles, peels, retinoids/acids on compromised skin | TEWL/irritation, symptom trajectory, infection or delayed healing |
| Diffuse volume loss | Consultation for anatomy and material choice | PLLA/CaHA/HA or surgery depending on goal | Treating an energy device as a substitute for volume restoration | Validated wrinkle/volume scale, adverse events, reversibility |

## 2. Combination rules

- **Foundational pair:** photoprotection + tolerable barrier care is compatible with nearly every lane and protects the endpoint being measured.
- **Do not equate adjunct with replacement:** a peptide, LED mask, or recovery serum may improve tolerability or add a modest signal without replacing an indication-specific therapy.
- **Separate irritation:** retinoids, exfoliating acids, benzoyl peroxide, strong vitamin C, peels, needles, heat, and laser are not all “actives”; they have different injury and recovery profiles.
- **Keep the measurement honest:** if several interventions start at once, an improvement cannot be attributed to one component.
