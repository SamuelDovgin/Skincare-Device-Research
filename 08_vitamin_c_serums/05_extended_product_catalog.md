# Extended Vitamin C Serum Catalog: 30+ Products Across All Tiers

*Compiled 2026-07-04. Prices are approximate retail; verify before purchasing. Products organized by vitamin C form and tier.*

## 0. How to read this catalog

This catalog expands the product comparison in [02_product_comparison.md](02_product_comparison.md) with many more options, organized by **active vitamin C form** and **price tier**. Each entry includes the vitamin C system, key supporting actives, packaging quality, and a practical verdict.

**Key signal to watch:** packaging type (airless pump > dropper), pH (for L-ascorbic acid), and whether the formula includes the stabilizing trio (vitamin E + ferulic acid).

## 11. Packaging Tier List (Vitamin C Stability)

| Tier | Packaging | Examples | Oxidation risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Best** | Opaque airless pump | Medik8 C-Tetra, IMAGE MD, Sunday Riley C.E.O., Biossance, BeautyStat | Lowest |
| **Good** | Opaque tube | CeraVe, LRP Pure Vitamin C, Kiehl's Powerful-Strength | Low |
| **Okay** | Opaque dropper bottle | Paula's Choice, Timeless, Maelove | Moderate — air enters with each use |
| **Worst** | Clear glass dropper | Many budget and older formulas | High — light + air exposure |

**Rule of thumb:** if your LAA serum is in a clear dropper and turning orange/brown, it's oxidizing. An airless pump can extend usable life significantly.
