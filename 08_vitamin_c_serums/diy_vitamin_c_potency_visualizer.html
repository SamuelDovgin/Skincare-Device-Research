        How to read the visualizer

        What the pie categories mean

        The chart begins with a modeled intact-LAA share. Any remainder is allocated to broad families: early oxidation (DHA), irreversible cleavage (DKG and small acids), acidic furan/carbonyl routes, higher-pH routes, and later colored condensation products. Individual molecules are not identified by the chart.

        Why the inputs matter

        The model uses temperature, pH, oxygen/headspace, light, container, water quality, concentration, and stabilizer multipliers. It is calibrated only as a transparent relative-pressure heuristic around a fresh acidic DIY batch. It does not extrapolate a purchased product’s preservative or packaging system.

        Model equation

        survival = exp(− days × pressure / 55) pressure = temperature × pH × light × air × container × water × concentration × stabilizer score = 100 × [0.72 × survival + 0.28 × (1 − color warning)] confidence band = score ± pH-measurement uncertainty

        The denominator and multipliers are planning assumptions, not a published shelf-life constant. Measurement confidence widens the displayed interpretation and can shorten the remake window; it does not chemically subtract from the central score. A separate, capped remake window also applies because chemical score cannot rule out microbial or formulation failure.

        Stability review ↗pH / browning study ↗Light / pH formulation study ↗Chelator mechanism ↗Ferulic stabilization study ↗
