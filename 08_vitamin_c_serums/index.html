Patent & Data Map

A compact claim chart for the patents and evidence anchors used throughout the vitamin C notes.

Expired foundationUS5140043A — Stable ascorbic acid compositions
Duke University · priority 1989-04-17 · low-pH L-ascorbic acid compositions
Early Duke anchor for topical L-ascorbic acid at pH no more than about 3.5 for UV-damage protection.
Expired CEF moatUS7179841B2 — Stabilized ascorbic acid compositions
Zielinski / Pinnell · L'Oreal/SkinCeuticals lineage · expired 2025-03-24
Single-phase low-pH L-ascorbic acid composition with cinnamic acid derivative such as ferulic acid, solvent system, water, and optional vitamin E.
Active adjacentUS9248082B2 — Anti-aging composition containing phloretin
SkinCeuticals/L'Oreal lineage · Phloretin CF anchor
Phloretin + L-ascorbic acid + ferulic acid antioxidant compositions; separate from the C E Ferulic patent family.
Derivative / L'OrealUS10231911B2 — Method and composition for treating skin conditions
L'Oreal SA · priority 2016-03-31
Includes high-percentage ascorbyl tetraisopalmitate in targeted booster compositions for brightness and pore-visibility use cases.
DerivativeUS7741496B2 — Ascorbic acid derivatives
Corum Inc · 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid
Describes 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid as a structurally stable ascorbic acid derivative and whitening agent.

Key Data Points

Classic delivery targetL-ascorbic acid, pH below 3.5, usually 10-20%.
C E Ferulic ratio15% L-ascorbic acid + 1% alpha tocopherol + 0.5% ferulic acid.
Ferulic resultDuke/SkinCeuticals paper reported vitamin C/E photoprotection rising from about 4-fold to about 8-fold.
Patent expiryUS7179841B2 expired on 2025-03-24, enabling closer legal dupes.
Routine timingVitamin C AM; Differin/adapalene PM; sunscreen remains the pigment anchor.
Open data gapTrader Joe's full INCI, pH, stability, and independent potency data still need verification.
