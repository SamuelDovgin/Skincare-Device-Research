## Exact Medicube Booster-H reports: vitamin C was tested

**Correction to the earlier search conclusion:** Medicube does publish a same-ampoule vitamin-C clinical comparison for older Booster-H. It measures skin outcomes, rather than the concentration of vitamin C reaching skin. It is separate from the Pro glutathione study and Mini Plus caffeine experiment.

| Report | Model, design and endpoint | What it found | Important limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S45 · GLB-220713-I1** | Booster-H; 22 women; four weeks. Deep Vita C Ampoule 2.0 on the whole face morning/night; device on left only, level 5, three minutes nightly. | Mean brightness baseline change **0.657% with device versus 0.272% product alone**; report states between-side p<.05. Hydration and several appearance measures also favored the device side. | APR-sponsored/internal institute; fixed side, no reported masking, multiple endpoints, short follow-up. Difference is **0.385 percentage points of baseline change**, not measured vitamin-C delivery or proof of a large visible change. |
| **S46 · GLB-220719-I1** | Booster-H; 22 women; one three-minute treatment with the same vitamin-C ampoule on both sides, left-side device. | Acute instrumental hydration and appearance measures favored the treated side; dermal-water signal changes **4.504% versus 2.491%**. | Acute cosmetic endpoints; does not identify vitamin-C concentration, durable pigment clearance or benefit with another serum. |
| **S47 · GLB-220727-I1** | Booster-H; 22 women; upper-arm comparison of ampoule plus device, ampoule alone and untreated area; one-minute device treatment. | At 24 hours, hydration baseline change **27.310% versus 12.689%**; reported device-versus-product p<.01. | Hydration of a multicomponent ampoule is not vitamin-C uptake. Side allocation and independence remain concerns. |
| **S48 · GLB-221101-I1** | Booster-H; 20 women; cream plus device versus cream alone; acute dry-weather exposure and sleep-mark measurements. | After one hour of drying, hydration baseline change **56.461% versus 34.538%**; reported p<.001. | Cream/hydration experiment; cannot be counted as a vitamin-C, high-molecular-weight delivery or long-term collagen trial. |

Primary manufacturer-released reports: [S45 four-week vitamin C](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0156/3905/2336/files/GLB-220713-I1_220826__-_Study_Report_ver.2.0_Dec_2021_____1.pdf?v=1671693686), [S46 acute vitamin C](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0156/3905/2336/files/GLB-220719-I1_220823__-_Study_Report_ver.2.1_Dec_2021_____1.pdf?v=1671693686), [S47 24-hour hydration](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0156/3905/2336/files/GLB-220727-I1_220823__-_Study_Report_ver.2.0_Dec_2021_____1.pdf?v=1671693686), [S48 cream/drying](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0156/3905/2336/files/GLB-221101-I1_221111__-_Study_Report_ver.3.0_Oct_2022_____1.pdf?v=1671693686). Reports carry restrictive reproduction notices; this archive preserves short assessments and canonical links, not redistributed report copies.

These reports support **C+ preliminary clinical evidence for Booster-H**, not B-level evidence for your finished formula or transferable proof for Pro/X2/Mini.

## Medicube Mini Plus and the high-molecular-weight question

**S40 · NCR-25501-PR065 excerpt, page 8/9:** the identified test compound is **caffeine**, approximately 194 Da. At 15-minute sampling, control was 16.6 and test 103.7 µg/mL, or **6.25×**; at four hours the ratio was **3.17×**. The statement identifies Mini Plus and TegoScience testing May 8–June 2, 2025. [Report excerpt](source_docs/medicube_report_excerpt_1.png) · [model-identifying statement](source_docs/medicube_report_excerpt_2.png).

The excerpt omits the complete skin model, sample count, exposure settings, dose and sampled compartment. Fifteen minutes is a sampling time, not confirmed treatment duration. It establishes a partial transport claim for Mini Plus, not a human face outcome or identical performance with vitamin C.

**No adequately documented exact-Medicube high-molecular-weight transport assay was recovered.** Manufacturer reading lists contain general macromolecule electroporation research; that is not a Medicube device test. Marketing paired with PDRN/collagen does not substitute for a matched transport experiment. The vitamin-C Booster-H clinical reports above do not answer the high-molecular-weight question either.

## YA-MAN: ingredient relevance, with generation boundaries

- **S50 · original Shiny M18-YL:** August 2022 human tape stripping, n=10 men, device OFF versus DYHP. Vitamin-C derivative VCPMG about **1.7×**, stratum corneum. This upgrades original M18 to C. **The Neo page expressly identifies M18 as the tested device.** Neo also has a small immediate subjective evaluation, which does not establish an exact-Neo clinical delivery increment. [Official footnote](https://www.ya-man-tokyo-japan.com/products/forface/photo-plus-shiny-neo_features.html).
- **S13 · Deep Lift YJFA1T:** manufacturer aqueous-ingredient forearm study, n=10 men, one minute, stratum-corneum endpoint; chart ratios approximately **1.7× vitamin-C derivative, 1.4× TXA, 1.7× niacinamide**. Direct ingredient relevance, limited finished-product/clinical translation. [Official study description](https://www.ya-man-tokyo-japan.com/products/forface/photo-plus-deep-lift.html).
- **S51 · Prestige SP M22:** public summary lists **1.7× vitamin-C derivative, 1.9× niacinamide and 1.4× TXA**, plus eight-week cosmetic changes. Adequate sample/control/protocol details were not recovered from the readable summary; these remain limited manufacturer claims. [Official international summary](https://global.ya-man.com/collections/all-products/products/photo-plus-prestige-sp).
- **S52–S57 · SP II, SP III, MIYABI, S, SS and Pro:** features/generation identities verified. No complete exact-model matched-formula clinical delivery trial was recovered and sufficiently reviewed for those models. A patent or “professional” frequency does not establish equal efficacy to a professional device. Each model has its own page and U grade.

RF studies involving other YA-MAN hardware or combined bundles belong to [the separate RF evidence map](../07_radio_frequency_skin_tightening/index.html). Their wrinkle/tightening findings cannot be counted as proof that these Photo Plus models enhance the clinical effect of your serum.

## Mechanisms and ordinary topical evidence

**S01–S03** establish that laboratory electroporation/sonophoresis can alter transport under specific electrical/acoustic conditions. They do not validate a retail wand’s waveform or serum outcome. [Prausnitz 1993](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC47805/), [Zewert 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10619443/), [Mitragotri 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7562407/).

**S21–S24 and S28–S29** inform the routine recommendation: controlled topical niacinamide/Thiamidol evidence, tazarotene and azelaic-acid labels, and formulation-specific vitamin-C penetration. These support ordinary topical use and irritation precautions; they are not device tests. [Full ingredient review and primary links](index.html#doc4).

**S41 · Lab Muffin:** Michelle Wong’s December 10, 2018 article discusses Panasonic EH-ST63, ion mechanisms and missing no-current controls. It was Panasonic-sponsored. No directly attributable Medicube-specific verdict was located in the searched public blog/YouTube/social indexes; inaccessible/private content prevents an exhaustive absence claim. [Her original article](https://labmuffin.com/ion-boosted-skincare-panasonic-skincare-tools-video/).

## Search limits and evidence accounting

The search used official model catalogues, manufacturer study lists, PubMed/publisher records, paper references and exact-model searches. Some publisher endpoints blocked extraction; lawful public paper mirrors were used where available. Image-only marketing and incomplete excerpts cannot support the same confidence as a complete protocol. Public commercial pages are dated snapshots; not every external URL was exhaustively rechecked.

Every recovered support item is mapped to its model, with access/capture status and checksums in the logs/manifest. A page with U or D grade records a real evidence gap rather than filling it with generic citations. This catalogue should be updated when a new exact-model report or your actual vitamin-C formulation becomes available.
