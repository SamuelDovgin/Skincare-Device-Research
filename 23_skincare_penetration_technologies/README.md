# Skincare penetration technologies: start here

Updated October 1, 2026. This section compares **ingredient delivery**, with RF tightening and EMS kept as separate purchasing goals.

**For your routine—vitamin C (assumed from “vitamin”), niacinamide, azelaic acid, Thiamidol, and tazarotene—buying no penetration device is the strongest value choice.** These are small molecules with established topical formulations. More uptake is not automatically more useful pigment or acne improvement. No convincing matched-formula, device-versus-no-device clinical comparison was located for this exact routine and these consumer devices.

If you want an optional experiment, a low-cost Medicube Mini/Mini Plus or full Booster Pro is easier to justify than an expensive high-output machine. Panasonic ST99/ST0A offers a well-documented, separate vitamin-C ion mode; its headline BOOST gains concern larger hydrophilic ingredients you rarely use. YA-MAN becomes more interesting when you also want its RF/EMS functions. Neither Panasonic’s HA multiples nor Medicube’s absorption percentages establish a clinical winner for your routine.
