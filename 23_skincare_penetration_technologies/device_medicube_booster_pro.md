Reviewed October 2, 2026. **Delivery research grade: C+**. This is the [public-evidence rubric](index.html#doc3), not a potency score. No reviewed model reaches A/B for this exact routine.

## What you are buying

Booster electroporation; microcurrent, Derma Shot EMS, Air Shot dry electric microporation, LED/sonic; no RF tightening mode. Specifications describe capability; they do not establish the amount of ingredient reaching a therapeutic target.

## Research supporting this exact model

Hur et al., 2023 exact-Pro trial:21 women in the EP arm, four weeks, 500 ppm glutathione ampoule both sides, left-side device 2 min twice daily at level 5. Preliminary pigment/brightness improvement; manufacturer authors, fixed side, no reported blinding, and unpaired between-side analyses.

Kwak et al., 2023 exact-Pro trial:44 women split into22 MC and22 EMS users, two weeks, matched ampoule on both sides. Reports appearance/lifting changes, with similar bias/statistics limitations. This adds support for extra modes, not absorption.

[Read both exact-Pro peer-reviewed studies](index.html#research). In the EP arm, mean L* rose63.438→64.316 on the device side versus63.188→63.695 on the product-only side: an incremental change of about0.371 L* units. Pigment index fell about 2% versus 1%. Statistical significance does not establish a clearly visible effect. The separate microcurrent/EMS paper is evidence for other modes.

## What this means for your skincare

My best balanced paid purchase: low price plus some exact-model human evidence involving another small active. Benefit for your vitamin-C/niacinamide products remains an inference.

For your vitamin C, niacinamide, azelaic acid, Thiamidol and tazarotene, only a compatible vitamin-C/niacinamide product is a plausible optional delivery experiment. The latter three do not have a located consumer-device trial showing useful added benefit. Tazarotene already has irritation risks; this archive does not recommend deliberately enhancing its delivery. Your exact vitamin-C form, pH and vehicle remain unknown. ## What remains unproven

No head-to-head study in this review establishes this device as the clinical winner against another brand. A trial of another generation, generic electroporation study, RF tightening result or high-molecular-weight ingredient assay does not fill the exact-model/formula gap. Where no complete study was recovered, that means limited accessible evidence, not proof of zero effect.

To change this recommendation, the useful evidence would be a randomized, masked, exact-device comparison using the same finished topical on both sides, with irritation and meaningful pigment/acne outcomes over adequate follow-up.
