DWHP (Dynamic Whitening Hyper Pulse)

In addition to our DYHP (Dynamic Hyper Pulse) Technology where the electrical pulses temporarily create open pathways in the skin, allowing serums and skincare ingredients to penetrate more effectively, our proprietary DWHP allows even more efficient absorption of brightening ingredients including high-molecular ingredients like collagen peptides.

Divided Electrodes

Experience professional-grade RF (Radio Frequency) at 1-3 MHz delivering just the right amount of heat to the deeper layers of your skin while the divided electrodes effectively stimulate the facial muscles with a complex yet comfortable rhythm for toned, lifted results.

Skin-Beautifying LED
