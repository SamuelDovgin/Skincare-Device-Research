6. What we can reasonably conclude
The Mini and original Pro may have similar electroporation output, but their equivalence is unverified.

Their five settings and similar treatment times do not demonstrate identical voltage or current. Their separate absorption claims also cannot reliably establish the difference.

The X2 has more direct evidence of increased electrical output.

Medicube reports independent power-density testing, a redesigned four-electrode head and a separate permeability experiment reaching 1,332% of control after one hour. The original Pro-to-X2 improvement still cannot be precisely quantified without matching protocols.

For your collagen-promoting skincare strategy, the missing experiment is straightforward: test all three devices on the same human skin samples, with the same peptide or other active ingredient, identical treatment durations, matched electrode contact conditions and measured delivery into different skin layers. Include untreated controls, dose-response measurements and a subsequent collagen-related biological endpoint.

At present, none of the advertised absorption percentages proves that one of these devices produces more collagen than another. The Mini may already deliver meaningful additional ingredient penetration, but paying for higher output on the X2 would be a bet on greater delivery rather than a demonstrated improvement in collagen banking.

Did LG investigate actual collagen?
Yes, but in cell culture rather than through measurements of new collagen in living people's faces.

In a separate experiment, LG's researchers used excised pig skin to investigate ingredient penetration and cultured human dermal fibroblasts to investigate collagen production.

When fibroblasts were exposed to a hyaluronic-acid-containing ampoule and combined electrical-ultrasound treatment, researchers reported increased cell proliferation and type I collagen production compared with their controls.

The LG papers are important because they examine both improved cosmetic delivery and biological effects. However, more extensive independently funded studies, including sham-device controls and human skin biopsies, would be needed to establish actual collagen banking.
