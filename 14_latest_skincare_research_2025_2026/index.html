What is actually new in skincare research?

The latest literature is converging on integrated skincare: photoprotection plus pigment control, acne treatment plus barrier support, procedures plus aftercare, and device comparisons that report recovery and pigment risk—not just efficacy.

Scope note: this is a reproducible 2025–2026 PubMed/MEDLINE evidence map, not a claim that every dermatology paper worldwide is included. The 100-record ledger preserves design, population, limitations, and conflict signals.
