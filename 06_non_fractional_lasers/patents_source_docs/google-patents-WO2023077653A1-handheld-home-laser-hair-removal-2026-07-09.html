Abstract

A handheld household laser hair removal instrument, which belongs to the field of laser hair removal, and comprises a hair removal instrument body, and a laser (1), an optical waveguide (2), a heat dissipation unit, a cooling unit and a controller that are disposed on the hair removal instrument body. The laser (1) comprises a substrate (11) and a plurality of laser chips (12) that are disposed on the substrate (11), and a laser beam emitted by the laser chips (12) is perpendicular to the substrate (11). The optical waveguide (2) is used for shaping the laser beam, and a treatment window (21) is formed on an end surface of the optical waveguide (2). The heat dissipation unit is used for dissipating heat, and the cooling unit is used for cooling the optical waveguide (2). The light exit direction of the laser beam is perpendicular to the substrate (11) which achieves the integration of a high-density two-dimensional area array, achieving the output of different powers or different wavelengths. The heat dissipation unit directly abuts against the substrate (11) for heat dissipation, and the heat dissipation unit has a simple structure. In addition, the optical waveguide (2) is directly cooled by using the cooling unit, and the end surface of the optical waveguide (2) is used as a treatment window (21) to cool the skin in a treatment region, so that the feeling of pain during hair removal may be relieved, and hair removal efficiency may be improved.

The device dissipates heat from the edge-emitting semiconductor laser. In addition, traditional household laser hair removal devices use metal treatment heads to cool the skin, and the temperature of the light treatment area is high. It is easy to burn the skin while removing hair. At the same time, the noise of the radiator is large, which affects the user experience.

而VCSEL激光器，出光方向垂直衬底，输出光斑为圆形，且具有低电压高电流的特点，可以在80℃下连续工作，不易损坏。并且传统家用激光脱毛仪基本采用通过切换档位治疗，相应的档位参数出厂时已经固定，无法满足客户定制化需求。On the other hand, the VCSEL laser has a light emitting direction perpendicular to the substrate, and the output spot is circular, and has the characteristics of low voltage and high current. It can work continuously at 80°C and is not easy to be damaged. Moreover, the traditional household laser hair removal device basically adopts the treatment by switching gears, and the corresponding gear parameters have been fixed at the factory, which cannot meet the customized needs of customers.

因此针对上述问题，本发明申请人旨在研发一种温度耐受高的家用激光脱毛仪，并通过制冷机构来减缓用户脱毛时的疼痛感，并满足用户定制化的要求。Therefore, in view of the above problems, the applicant of the present invention aims to develop a household laser hair removal instrument with high temperature tolerance, and use a refrigeration mechanism to relieve the pain of the user during hair removal, and to meet the user's customization requirements.
