Description

Household laser speckle-removing and anti-aging beauty instrument
Technical Field
The invention relates to the technical field of laser treatment equipment, in particular to a household laser speckle-removing and anti-aging beauty instrument.
Background
With the rapid development of society, people's life pressure is bigger and bigger, and with the increase of age and stay up and overtime, facial skin is loose and loose, and the unhealthy of diet and injury from environment (ultraviolet ray, air pollution, etc.) cause skin aging, and skin aging easily appears symptoms such as french, angular pocket, unclear mandible, double chin, apple muscle move down.
The domestic skin tendering instrument which is common in the market at present adopts an IPL photon skin tendering instrument, and the (IPL) strong pulse light or pulse strong light is a broad spectrum light formed by focusing and filtering a light source with high intensity, and the nature of the broad spectrum light is incoherent ordinary light rather than laser.
In order to penetrate deeper layers of skin, the energy generated by the IPL needs to be increased, so that a high-power IPL is needed, but the corresponding skin tenderer is designed to be larger in size and becomes more bulky, which makes the treatment of facial skin very inconvenient and unsuitable for household use.

Detailed Description
The application is described in further detail below with reference to fig. 1-7.
The embodiment of the application discloses a household laser speckle-removing and anti-aging beauty instrument. Referring to fig. 1 to 7, the beauty treatment instrument comprises a housing 1, a treatment head 2, an optical waveguide 3, an optical waveguide cover 4, a laser 5, a radiator 6, a fan 7 and a circuit board 8, wherein the treatment head 2 is installed at one end of the housing 1, the optical waveguide cover 4 is installed in the housing 1, the optical waveguide 3 is installed in the optical waveguide cover 4, the laser 5 is installed in the optical waveguide cover 4, the radiator 6 is installed in the housing 1, the radiator 6 is used for radiating the laser 5, the fan 7 is installed in the radiator 6, the circuit board 8 is installed at the other end of the housing 1, and the circuit board 8 is electrically connected with the fan 7.
When skin treatment is carried out, the circuit board 8 controls the laser 5 to emit laser, the laser passes through the optical waveguide 3, the optical waveguide 3 gathers the laser, then the gathered laser passes through the treatment head 2 to reach the skin, and under the same volumes of the laser 5 and the IPL, the energy generated by the laser is larger than that generated by the IPL, so that the laser 5 can generate higher energy to treat the skin in a deeper level without increasing the volume of the shell 1, the fan 7 blows the heat radiator 6, and the air flow takes away the heat transferred from the heat radiator 6 to dissipate the heat of the treatment head 2 and the laser 5.
The shell 1 comprises an upper shell 11, a lower shell 12 and a front shell 13, wherein the side edge of the upper shell 11 is buckled with the side edge of the lower shell 12, an extension shell 121 is integrally formed on the outer side surface of one end of the lower shell 12, the side edge of the front shell 13 is buckled on the side edge of the extension shell 121, the side edge of the end part of the front shell 13 is buckled on the side edge of the end part of the upper shell 11, and a mounting opening 14 is formed between the extension shell 121 and the inner wall of the end part of the front cover far away from the upper shell 11.
The treatment head 2 is also called an aluminum head and mainly made of aluminum alloy, the treatment head 2 comprises a mounting ring 21 and a connecting shell 22, the mounting ring 21 is tightly pressed on the end faces of the extending shell 121 and the front shell 13 and is clamped on the inner walls of the extending shell 121 and the front shell 13, one end of the connecting shell 22 is integrally formed on the side face of the mounting ring 21, which is close to the extending shell 121, the outer peripheral face of the connecting shell 22 is attached to the inner peripheral face of the extending shell 121, the connecting shell 22 is connected to the inner wall of the extending shell 121, a lens 23 is embedded on the side face of the mounting ring 21, which is close to the connecting shell 22, laser passes through the lens 23, four mounting holes 211 are formed in the mounting ring 21, the four mounting holes 211 are arranged around the mounting ring 21 in a circle, an insulating sleeve 24 is embedded on the inner wall of the mounting holes 211, a conductive post 25 is penetrated in the insulating sleeve 24, the conductive post 25 is a copper post, the side face of the mounting ring 21, which is close to the connecting shell 22, the side face of the mounting ring 21, which is close to the annular circuit board 26, is coated with an insulating layer, the end of the conductive post 25 is connected to the annular circuit board 26, and the end of the conductive post 25 is far from the skin of a user.
When the skin tendering device is used, the treatment head 2 is close to the skin of a user, when the four guide posts 25 are contacted with the skin, the guide posts 25 transmit signals to the annular circuit board 26, and the annular circuit board 26 controls the laser 5 to emit laser light, and if the four guide posts are not contacted with the skin, the laser 5 does not emit laser light.
The optical waveguide cover body 4 comprises an optical waveguide upper cover 41 and an optical waveguide lower cover 42, the optical waveguide upper cover 41 is connected with the optical waveguide lower cover 42, the laser 5 is arranged between the optical waveguide upper cover 41 and the optical waveguide lower cover 42, the optical waveguide upper cover 41 and the optical waveguide lower cover 42 seal the laser 5, the emitting end of the laser 5 is rectangular, a plurality of parallel light beams are emitted in the laser 5, all the parallel light beams are located on the same plane, one section of the optical waveguide 3 is arranged on the inner wall of the connecting shell 22, the other section of the optical waveguide 3 is arranged on the inner wall of the optical waveguide upper cover 41, the optical waveguide 3 is arranged between the laser 5 and the lens 23, one end of the optical waveguide 3 is rectangular, the other end of the optical waveguide 3 is circular, the rectangular end face of the optical waveguide 3 is tightly attached to the rectangular end face of the laser 5, and the circular end face of the optical waveguide 3 is aligned with the lens 23.
The laser 5 is wrapped by the optical waveguide upper cover 41 and the optical waveguide lower cover 42, dust is prevented from contacting the laser 5 to pollute the laser 5, and parallel light beams generated by the laser 5 are converted into circular light spots through the optical waveguide 3 by means of the shape setting of the optical waveguide 3, so that laser is more concentrated, the design size of the treatment head 2 is relatively compact, and the treatment of facial skin is facilitated.
The radiator 6 is made of aluminum alloy, the radiator 6 comprises an upper base 61, a lower base 62, a first heat pipe 63, a second heat pipe 64, a first radiating fin group 65 and a second radiating fin group 66, wherein the upper base 61 and the lower base 62 are respectively arranged in the shell 1, the first heat pipe 63 and the second heat pipe 64 are uniformly formed on the same side surface of the lower base 62, the first heat pipe 63 and the second heat pipe 64 are arranged in parallel, the fin uniform body of the first radiating fin group 65 is connected with the peripheral surface of the first heat pipe 63, the fin uniform body of the second radiating fin group 66 is connected with the peripheral surface of the second heat pipe 64, the first heat pipe 63 and the second heat pipe 64 are uniformly formed on the same side surface of the upper base 61, the fan 7 is inserted between the first radiating fin group 65 and the second radiating fin group 66, the side surface of the upper base 61 is arranged on a mounting groove 611, the optical waveguide upper cover 41 is connected with the side surface of the upper base 62, the optical waveguide lower cover 42 is arranged on the inner wall of the mounting groove, the top surface of the upper cover 5 is positioned on the peripheral surface of the lower cover 62 and is attached to the lower base 62, the other end surface of the upper cover 5 is attached to the upper base 3, and the laser waveguide cover 4 is attached to the upper base 3, and the other end of the upper cover 61 is attached to the upper base 3.
When the laser 5 is radiating, the laser 5 transfers heat to the upper base 61, the upper base 61 transfers heat to the first radiating fin group 65 and the second radiating fin group 66 through the first heat pipe 63 and the second heat pipe 64, and the fan 7 blows away the heat, so that the laser 5 can be cooled.
The end surface of the connection shell 22 far away from the mounting ring 21 is provided with a placement groove 221, a TEC (semiconductor refrigerator) 27 is installed in the placement groove 221, one end of the connection shell 22 far away from the mounting ring 21 is connected to the side surface of the upper base 61, the TEC27 is attached to the end surface of the upper base 61 close to the connection shell 22, and the first heat pipe 63 is located on one side of the second heat pipe 64 close to the connection shell 22.
The TEC27 performs heat transfer between the connection housing 22 and the upper base 61, the fan 7 blows the first heat radiation fin group 65, the first heat radiation fin group 65 achieves cooling of the upper base 61 through the heat pipe effect of the first heat pipe 63, and heat transfer is performed between the upper base 61 and the connection housing 22, so that the temperature of the connection housing 22 is reduced, and because the installation ring 21 and the connection housing 22 are integrally designed, the temperature of the installation ring 21 is also reduced, so that when the installation ring 21 and the lens 23 contact the skin, the freezing point effect is achieved, and the treatment is more comfortable.
The first radiating fin group 65 and the second radiating fin group 66 are formed by splicing a plurality of fins, airflow flowing gaps are formed between the adjacent fins, the upper shell 11, the lower shell 12 and the front shell 13 are streamline, the side edges of the fins of the first radiating fin group 65 are arc-shaped and are attached to the inner side surface of the lower cover, the fins of the second radiating fin group 66 are arc-shaped and are attached to the inner side surface of the upper shell 11, the inner space of the shell 1 is reasonably utilized, the size of the fins is designed to be maximum, the maximum radiating capacity can be provided, the air outlet 111 is formed in the part, close to the second radiating fin group 66, of the upper shell 11, the air inlet 122 is formed in the part, close to the first radiating fin group 65, of the lower shell 12, the fan 7 is inserted between the first radiating fin group 6 and the second radiating fin group 66, the structure is more compact, the fins with larger sizes are convenient to design, and rough protrusions are arranged on the surfaces of the fins of the first radiating fin group 65 and the second radiating fins.
