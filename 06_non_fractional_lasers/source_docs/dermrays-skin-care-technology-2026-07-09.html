Frequently Asked Questions
What are the differences between DermRays Revive Laser and LED Mask?

Technically, they differ in their light sources. The DermRays Revive Laser employs a diode laser, whereas the DermRays LED Mask utilizes light emitting diodes (LEDs). They also vary in wavelength; the Revive Laser operates at a single wavelength of 1064nm, while the LED Mask offers multiple wavelengths: 460nm, 620nm, and 850nm. Moreover, the energy density of the Revive Laser is significantly higher compared to LED.

These technical disparities determine their functional distinctions. The LED Mask is suitable for overall daily skincare routines. If your skincare needs are vague or you simply aim to brighten your complexion or improve oiliness, then the LED Mask is more suitable. Conversely, if you seek targeted solutions for specific skin issues, especially for quick results such as pigmentation reduction, then the Revive Laser is more appropriate.

However, we highly recommend purchasing both to benefit from our "BUY 2 SAVE $200" promotion. This combination not only facilitates daily maintenance for the entire face but also provides targeted solutions for various issues, achieving comprehensive anti-aging benefits.

Are DermRays Revive Laser and LED Mask safe to use?

Absolutely. Both of our products have undergone rigorous certification processes from the US FDA, UKCA, and European CE. Quality assurance measures are also implemented before they leave the factory, ensuring your safety and peace of mind during usage.

How soon can I see results?

Typically, significant changes are observed after adhering to the treatment regimen for at least 4 weeks. However, individual results may vary depending on skin condition and treatment frequency.

Nonetheless, we confidently present specific clinical data: DermRays Revive Laser, after undergoing strict third-party clinical testing by SGS with 28 subjects, demonstrated remarkable results within 28 days of following the prescribed regimen. Users reported 86% fewer pigmentation spots, 82% brighter skin tone, 86% tighter pores, and 86% smoother skin. Moreover, users experienced a reduction of 7.90% in skin melanin content, an improvement of 8.63% in pigmentation, and a 12.26% increase in elasticity.

What is the recommended frequency of use?

For DermRays Revive Laser, we recommend the following frequency:

  • 3-4 times per week for the first 2 weeks
  • 2-3 times per week for the subsequent 3 weeks
  • Once or twice a month thereafter


DermRays LED Mask can be used daily, adjusted as needed.

If you purchase both, alternate their use every other day for optimal results.

Additionally, you can adjust the frequency of use based on individual needs, considering differences in skin type, treatment goals, and energy levels.

Can I use DermRays Revive Laser and LED Mask if my skin is sensitive?

Yes, both DermRays Revive Laser and LED Mask are safe for all skin types, including sensitive skin. For most users, the treatment process is virtually painless. For first-time users of the Revive Laser, we recommend starting at level 1 and gradually increasing the energy as tolerated.
