# Non-Fractional Laser Device Landscape

This is an initial map of **real-laser or laser-adjacent devices that are not fractional resurfacing devices**. The goal is to keep "laser" claims from collapsing into one category.

Confidence: ✅ verified / ⚠️ marketing claim / 🔍 inferred.

## 2. DermRays Revive: the anchor

DermRays Revive is the clearest reason to make this folder:

- ✅ **FDA:** K231910, applicant **Wuhan Lotuxs Technology Co., Ltd.**
- ✅ **Device class/product code:** powered laser surgical instrument, product code **GEX**
- ✅ **Use type:** **Prescription use**
- ✅ **Specs:** 1064nm +/- 10nm, 400 ms pulse width, 5.0-10.0 J/cm2, 15mm spot, 1.766 cm2 working area
- ✅ **Indications:** hair removal, permanent hair reduction, and wrinkles
- ⚠️ **Marketing:** pigment, melasma, redness, glow, lift, firmness, "3mm penetration" style claims

**Verdict:** legitimate FDA-cleared laser hardware, but it is not an OTC fractional-resurfacing device. Treat it as a **non-fractional 1064nm bulk-heating/Rx laser** with interesting but under-documented home-use claims.

## 3. 810nm wide-window devices

DermRays V8S/V4S and CurrentBody-style 810nm devices remain mostly a **hair-removal** story:

- lower fluence than Tria, but much bigger window and corded convenience;
- more realistic for body coverage;
- not useful as a pigment/tone solution;
- not fractional resurfacing.

## 4. NIRA and other non-fractional 1450nm warmers

NIRA is a useful boundary case. It uses a legitimate wrinkle-reduction wavelength family, but not fractional microbeams:

- ✅ FDA-cleared device family;
- ✅ 1450nm non-ablative/non-fractional;
- ✅ home-use positioning;
- ❌ not a Clear + Brilliant analog;
- ❌ weak pigment rationale.

NIRA belongs in this folder conceptually, but it is also retained in the fractional-laser landscape as an **adjacent comparator** because buyers often cross-shop it against Tria FRX.

## 5. LYMA / LLLT devices

LYMA uses the word "laser," but its mechanism is low-level photobiomodulation, not thermal remodeling. It belongs near LED/PBM in terms of expected effect size, not near DermRays Revive or in-office Nd:YAG.

## 6. Professional 1064nm context

The main professional reference class is long-pulsed 1064nm Nd:YAG:

- commonly used for darker-skin hair removal because epidermal melanin absorption is lower than shorter wavelengths;
- can target deeper vascular structures;
- appears in predicate language for pigmented/vascular lesions and wrinkles;
- typically has cooling, trained operators, and far more flexible power/spot settings than home devices.

**Important distinction:** DermRays Revive can cite a 1064nm professional predicate and still not produce equivalent outcomes. Predicate equivalence is not an outcomes guarantee.

## 7. Buyer's ranking by goal

| Goal | Better lane |
|---|---|
| Dark/coarse hair, small areas | Tria 4X/Precision |
| Dark/coarse hair, large areas | DermRays V8S/V4S / wide-window 810nm or in-office |
| Wrinkle maintenance with low downtime | NIRA or RF, depending tolerance and commitment |
| Experimental deeper 1064nm home laser | DermRays Revive |
| Pigment/PIH primary goal | Not this folder: SPF/topicals + in-office 1927nm/vascular/pigment work |
| Clear + Brilliant-like resurfacing | Fractional folder only |
