A moderate-strength acid, not a peel-strength shortcut

Glycolic acid has real human evidence for texture and discoloration, plus limited remodeling evidence. The practical decision is formulation and tolerance—not chasing the highest percentage.

My starting target: a 5% leave-on glycolic emulsion/cream with pH around 3.5–4.0, humectants, and soothing/barrier-supportive ingredients. Keep it off Tria and retinoid nights at first.

  4. Trial one product on a non-Tria, non-retinoid night.
