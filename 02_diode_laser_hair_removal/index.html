Utility PatentUS 5,735,844 A — Hair removal using optical pulses
Palomar / MGH — The General Hospital Corporation (Massachusetts General Hospital); exclusively licensed to Palomar Medical Technologies in 1995.
Filed 1996-01-30 · Granted 1998-04-07
The foundational selective-photothermolysis hair-removal patent — beam ≥8mm, 0.75–1cm² treatment area, fluence 10–200 J/cm² (preferred 30–50), sapphire contact cooled 4–15°C, 680–1200nm melanin-selective. Licensed to Cynosure, Candela, Syneron, Laserscope, and Cutera — the most consequential patent in hair-removal licensing history. (Also cited in [01_ipl_hair_removal](../01_ipl_hair_removal/06_final_recommendation.md).)

Utility PatentUS 7,118,563 B2 — Self-contained, diode-laser-based dermatologic treatment apparatus
Tria Beauty — originally SpectraGenics, Inc.; inventors include Robert E. Grove, who co-developed the original clinical LightSheer diode laser at Coherent/Lumenis in 1993 before founding SpectraGenics.
Filed 2004-02-19 · Granted 2006-10-10 — **Expired** (2025-03-19)
~800nm diode, laser hair removal/hair-regrowth inhibition, fluence 10–40 J/cm². Tria's direct engineering ancestor — turned the clinical LightSheer concept into a self-contained, battery-powered home device.

Utility PatentUS 7,250,045 B2 — Self-contained, eye-safe hair-regrowth-inhibition apparatus and method
Tria Beauty — SpectraGenics, Inc. Inventors Tobin C. Island, Robert E. Grove, Mark V. Weckwerth.
Filed 2004-02-19 · Granted 2007-07-31 — **Expired** (2024-02-19)
Spot size 0.25–5cm² (preferred ~0.8cm²), fluence 4–100 J/cm² (preferred ~20), sapphire output window thermally bonded to a heat-removal element, AlGaAs laser diode bars at ~800nm with an optical diffuser for eye safety, battery-powered/direct-drive/self-contained. The clearest "home hair-removal device" patent in this whole search.
