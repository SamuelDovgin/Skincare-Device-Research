## Evidence status

The Tria manuals provide direct device-specific instructions and manufacturer-submitted outcome summaries. They do not eliminate the need for independent comparative trials, long-term durability measurement, exact used-unit output testing, or skin-tone-stratified safety data. The new progress audit makes those limitations visible instead of treating a favorable short-term photo as proof of permanent reduction.
