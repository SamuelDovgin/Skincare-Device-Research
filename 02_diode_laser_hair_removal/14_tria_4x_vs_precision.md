# Tria 4X vs Tria Precision: Same Laser Dose, Different Workflow

**Compiled:** 2026-07-10  
**Question:** Are the Tria 4X and discontinued Tria Precision essentially the same device apart from battery life?

## Bottom line

**Your understanding is mostly right about treatment strength, but the devices are not identical in every published specification.** Both manuals specify an **810 nm diode laser** and the same **7–20 J/cm² output-fluence range**. There is no manual evidence that the 4X delivers a higher maximum dose per square centimeter. [[1]](Tria_Precision_IFU_HRLp.pdf)[[2]](Tria_4X_IFU_2ndGen_current.pdf)

The 4X advantage is mainly workflow: about **30 minutes per full charge** versus the Precision manual's **15 minutes**, five treatment levels instead of three, an LCD/pulse-counting interface, a larger full-hand grip, and a 2 A rather than 1 A charger. [[1]](Tria_Precision_IFU_HRLp.pdf)[[2]](Tria_4X_IFU_2ndGen_current.pdf)[[3]](https://www.trialaser.com/products/hair-removal-laser-4x) Both are cordless-only during treatment.

The careful wording is therefore: **same published wavelength and maximum fluence; likely the same per-spot treatment class, but not proven identical internally.** Only the Precision manual publishes 40 W maximum laser radiation and a 400 ms maximum pulse duration. The 4X manuals do not publish directly comparable wattage or pulse-duration values, so “same power and all specs” goes beyond the available evidence. [[1]](Tria_Precision_IFU_HRLp.pdf)[[2]](Tria_4X_IFU_2ndGen_current.pdf)

## What “same strength” does and does not establish

Fluence is the relevant published per-area dose measure, so the matching 20 J/cm² ceiling is strong evidence that Precision is **not a deliberately weaker mini model** at its highest setting. It is reasonable to expect both to occupy the same efficacy class on a small patch when used at the highest tolerated setting, on suitable skin and dark hair, and with a healthy battery.

However, equal wavelength and fluence do not prove identical optical spot size, pulse shape, thermal controls, internal diode package, repetition rate, or level-by-level dose mapping. Tria does not disclose enough current 4X engineering detail to verify those as identical. This comparison therefore avoids calling the devices hardware clones.

Also, charger amperage is not treatment power. The 4X's 5 V / 2 A charger can replenish energy faster than the Precision's 5 V / 1 A charger, but neither device operates while connected to its charger, and charger input cannot be converted into laser-output wattage. [[1]](Tria_Precision_IFU_HRLp.pdf)[[2]](Tria_4X_IFU_2ndGen_current.pdf)

## Practical buying decision

Choose **Precision** when the price is much lower and the target is a genuinely small area—underarms, bikini edges, or a small patch—and the used battery demonstrates close to its stated runtime. Its three levels still reach the same published 20 J/cm² ceiling.

Choose **4X** for repeated or larger-area work. The roughly doubled runtime, finer five-level adjustment, pulse counter, grip, current warranty/support, and more durable current housing are meaningful adherence advantages. [[2]](Tria_4X_IFU_2ndGen_current.pdf)[[3]](https://www.trialaser.com/products/hair-removal-laser-4x)[[4]](https://www.trialaser.com/pages/user-manuals) They make treatment less tedious; they do not establish a stronger maximum laser dose.

For a used unit, battery health can outweigh the model name. A worn 4X that stops early may be less useful than a healthy Precision. Test the unit unplugged, verify that it unlocks, cycles all levels, fires repeatedly, and holds charge before paying.

| Conclusion | Confidence | Basis / gap |
|---|---|---|
| Same 810 nm wavelength and 7–20 J/cm² fluence range | **High** | Printed in both primary manuals [[1]](Tria_Precision_IFU_HRLp.pdf)[[2]](Tria_4X_IFU_2ndGen_current.pdf) |
| 4X lasts roughly twice as long per charge | **Moderate-high** | Precision manual says 15 min; current official 4X FAQ says around 30 min [[1]](Tria_Precision_IFU_HRLp.pdf)[[3]](https://www.trialaser.com/products/hair-removal-laser-4x) |
| 4X is stronger per spot | **Not supported** | Same published fluence ceiling |
| Internals, pulse shape, spot size, and peak wattage are identical | **Unknown** | 4X IFU omits the comparable engineering values |
| Equal small-area results at matched settings | **Reasonable inference, not a head-to-head finding** | Same wavelength/fluence class, but no direct Precision-vs-4X trial located |
