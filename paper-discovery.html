A REPEATABLE SEARCH ROUTINE

From a topic to a useful reading stack

01 / Cast a wider net

Run the main topic and a few alternative phrasings. Leave access unrestricted. Check the search log for caps and failures; split large topics into narrower questions.

02 / Follow the trail

Pick a relevant review or landmark paper. Import its references and papers citing it, then repeat on useful discoveries. Citation batches are leads to screen, not endorsements.

03 / Locate full text

Try listed free links, the DOI/publisher page, and an exact-title search. Check author or university repositories, your library, interlibrary loan, or ask the author. Add your own link or filename and mark “Copy obtained.”

04 / Read and annotate

Keep, maybe, exclude, or mark read. Record study design, sample, comparator, outcome, limitations, and your questions in notes. Export CSV for analysis or RIS for Zotero; back up JSON to preserve the entire workspace.

Coverage, privacy, and source documentation

Searches go directly from your browser to the selected providers. Notes and file references stay in browser storage and your exports; this tool does not upload or store PDFs. A filename is a reminder of where you saved a file, not an attached copy. Free links are provider-reported leads, not a guarantee of current access or reuse rights. An unresolved record may still be freely available elsewhere.

Europe PMC focuses on life sciences and supplies indexed publication types and abstracts where available. Crossref broadens discovery across disciplines, including non-paper records and sparse metadata. Crossref relevance search is not Boolean search; advanced Europe PMC syntax disables Crossref. Citation coverage is incomplete and depends on the source. Search-result order and journal labels are not quality ratings. Preprints, corrections, and retractions may appear: verify the current record before relying on a paper.

Deduplication uses DOI, PMID, source ID, then conservative title/year matching. Different DOIs stay separate, so review preprint/published versions yourself. Automatic merges preserve your existing notes. Reimporting a backup merges missing papers and provenance, keeping existing local screening and notes for duplicates.

Implementation references (checked September 6, 2026): Europe PMC API, Europe PMC search syntax, Crossref metadata API, and Crossref access and limits.
