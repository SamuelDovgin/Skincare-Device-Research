## Buying Thesis

For facial barrier experimentation, the best first product is not the strongest zinc oxide paste. It is the product with the best balance of:

- 10-15% zinc oxide,
- fragrance-free formula,
- minimal obvious facial allergens,
- no loose powder or spray format,
- tolerable texture,
- current official label support.

That makes **12.8-14% zinc oxide** the best starting range. 40% can be useful, but it is a patch-rescue tool, not the first full-face experiment.

## Concentration Guide

| Zinc oxide | Product style | Facial read |
|---:|---|---|
| 1-5% | Light skin protectant / mixed moisturizer | Probably too light if chasing the diaper-cream barrier effect |
| 10-15% | Daily diaper cream, face/body rash ointment, lighter barrier cream | Best first range for a face experiment |
| 20-25% | Heavier rash cream | Reasonable for small irritated patches |
| 40% | Maximum strength paste | Patch rescue only; not a default full-face night product |

## Products I Would Not Choose First For The Face

| Product/category | Why not first |
|---|---|
| Fragranced 40% diaper pastes | More irritation/allergy variables than needed |
| Botanical-heavy/calendula/essential-oil diaper creams | Pleasant branding, but unnecessary sensitization surface for facial use |
| Lanolin-heavy formulas if sensitive | Lanolin can be very useful on body skin and still be a bad fit for allergy-prone damaged facial skin |
| Zinc oxide powder | Loose powder is hard to dose evenly and creates avoidable inhalation/mess risk |
| Zinc pyrithione products | Different zinc compound and use case; not a diaper-rash zinc oxide barrier cream |
| Mineral sunscreen as night paste | Sunscreen formulas are built and tested for UV labeling, not overnight barrier rescue |

## Selection Checklist

Before buying, check the label for:

1. **Active ingredient:** zinc oxide, ideally 10-15% for first facial test.
2. **Purpose:** skin protectant.
3. **Fragrance:** avoid if possible.
4. **Lanolin:** avoid if you have allergy/sensitivity history.
5. **Botanicals/resins:** minimize for face.
6. **Seller:** prefer brand store or reliable Amazon source.
7. **Packaging:** choose a tube if possible; tubs are economical but less hygienic.
8. **Return/currentness:** product pages can merge variants. Confirm the exact label when it arrives.
