# LDM mechanism and the HIFU distinction

*Compiled October 9, 2026. Verified platform descriptions, manufacturer hypotheses and research interpretation are separated.*

## Bottom line

**LDM means local dynamic micromassage: therapeutic ultrasound that switches rapidly among frequencies. It should have its own evidence lane rather than inherit the outcomes of HIFU, RF or any other multi-frequency device.** [[1]](https://www.wellcomet.de/html/page.php?lan=de&page_id=38)

## What is actually delivered?

Wellcomet describes dual combinations such as 1/3 and 3/10 MHz, and triple combinations of 1/3/10 and 3/10/19 MHz. Frequency is the acoustic oscillation rate; the alternation schedule is a separate variable. A label listing several frequencies does not reveal switching timing, intensity, duty cycle or calibrated acoustic output. [[1]](https://www.wellcomet.de/html/page.php?lan=de&page_id=38)

The manufacturer proposes effects on matrix metalloproteinases, heat shock proteins and hyaluronic acid. These are mechanism claims, not a measured collagen percentage for an individual patient. Hydration, turgor and visible smoothing are different endpoints from durable tissue remodelling. [[2]](https://www.wellcomet.de/html/page.php?lan=en&page_id=65)

**Section finding — S01/S03:** official documentation establishes what the manufacturer describes. It does not independently validate each biological explanation or clinical claim.

## Compare the ultrasound lanes

| Lane | Research target | What must be verified |
|---|---|---|
| LDM / alternating therapeutic ultrasound | Skin quality and inflammatory-skin endpoints | Exact generator, handpiece, sequence, acoustic intensity, coupling and study indication |
| HIFU / microfocused ultrasound | Procedure-specific focal heating and tightening | Focal geometry, depth, anatomy, imaging/control and operator protocol; see the [existing HIFU research](../11_hifu_skin_tightening/index.html#doc1) |
| Other dual-frequency ultrasound | Outcomes of that particular system | Equivalence to LDM must be demonstrated, not assumed from MHz |
| Ultrasound-assisted delivery | Transport of a particular formulation | Compatible formulation and measured uptake; see [delivery research](../23_skincare_penetration_technologies/index.html#doc2) |

This comparison is a research classification, not a claim that every device in a category uses the same physics or produces the same outcome.

## Frequency is not a precise treatment depth

The manufacturer gives approximate half-depths of 3 mm at 10 MHz and 1.5 mm at 19 MHz. A half-depth describes attenuation; it is not a hard boundary, an HIFU focal point, or a guarantee of targeting a single skin layer. Tissue properties and the complete exposure matter. [[2]](https://www.wellcomet.de/html/page.php?lan=en&page_id=65)

**Section finding — S03:** these are manufacturer approximations. This section intentionally has no depth-to-collagen or frequency-to-result calculator.

## Read the clinical endpoints first

Ask whether a paper measured redness, TEWL (water loss through skin), acne severity, wrinkles, or actual tissue structure. A change in one cannot simply be relabelled as all the others. The [evidence guide](index.html#doc2) records those distinctions.

## Evidence gaps

Independent acoustic-output comparisons, a common-protocol LDM-versus-HIFU trial, and direct long-term human collagen measurements for current platforms were not established in this initial corpus. The exact IFU remains necessary for clinical use.
