## Preserved evidence

See the [rendered capture manifest](index.html#doc5) for local files and the [machine-readable checksums](source_docs/capture_manifest.json) for integrity. The [study ledger](data/studies.csv) keeps designs, populations, endpoints and limitations together. The [research recovery log](source_docs/research_resource_log_2026-10-09.txt) records source IDs, access status, preservation details and the sections using each source.

PMC's acne page presented a browser challenge during direct retrieval; the paper was read from an openly licensed PDF. The 2018 source is an abstract/metadata record, not an archived full paper. Do not confuse metadata with full-text access.
