# LDM ultrasound: local dynamic micromassage

*Compiled October 9, 2026. Evidence orientation for skin-quality and inflammatory-skin questions.*

## Start here

**LDM deserves its own section: it is a rapidly alternating, multi-frequency therapeutic ultrasound lane, distinct from the focused-ultrasound lifting procedures in the HIFU archive.** Wellcomet uses the LDM name for its technology; a generic “ultrasound facial” or home wand is not automatically the same system. [[1]](https://www.wellcomet.de/html/page.php?lan=de&page_id=38)

The reviewed human literature offers preliminary signals for redness/barrier dysfunction, acne and cosmetic skin appearance. It does not establish a best home LDM device, a predictable collagen increase, or superiority to established treatments. The [human evidence guide](index.html#doc2) separates actual LDM studies from adjacent ultrasound evidence.

## Where LDM fits

For redness or barrier concerns, start with the evidence rather than a clinic's “water-drop lifting” name. For meaningful lifting or structural collagen remodelling, compare endpoints with the [HIFU](../11_hifu_skin_tightening/index.html), [RF](../07_radio_frequency_skin_tightening/index.html), [microneedling](../10_microneedling_collagen_induction/index.html) and [Asian-device comparison](../24_asian_devices_and_collagen_remodelling/index.html) archives.
