# LDM devices, claims and selection questions

*Compiled October 9, 2026. Official specifications are dated snapshots; no current price ranking or purchase recommendation.*

## Bottom line

**Identify the exact device before assessing an LDM offer. A multi-frequency home wand, an OEM “LDM” label, and a studied Wellcomet platform require separate evidence.** This initial section establishes the professional reference and the verification questions; it does not certify lookalikes.

## Regulatory and safety status

An official product page is a claim/specification source, not a clearance database or IFU. **This pass has not verified an exact-model FDA clearance, complete IFU contraindication list, or home-use authorization for LDM.** That is an open verification gap, not a statement that no authorization exists anywhere.

Before any procedure, the provider should use the current exact-model IFU to check the indication, treatment site, relevant medical conditions, pregnancy considerations, implants, recent procedures and concurrent treatments. This is a checklist of questions, not a sourced universal contraindication list. Research-paper settings are not a home protocol.

## Questions to ask before paying

1. What is the manufacturer, exact model, serial identity and handpiece? Is “LDM” being used as a brand/technology name or just a service label?
2. Which study used this model and measured my outcome? Redness, hydration, wrinkles and lifting require different evidence.
3. What is the local authorized indication, and may I inspect the current IFU and credentialed operator's plan?
4. What is the full-course cost, reassessment point and maintenance proposal? What result would count as enough benefit to continue?
5. How are intensity, duty cycle, coupling/contact and servicing controlled? What happens if symptoms worsen?

**Section finding:** these are evidence-audit questions derived from the gaps in the [study map](index.html#doc2), not a ranking of providers or a clinical prescription.

## Goal-specific expectations

| Goal | Research interpretation |
|---|---|
| Redness / barrier | An adjunct to discuss after diagnosis; related studies are promising but uncontrolled |
| Acne | Preliminary model-specific observational support; no superiority or treatment-replacement verdict |
| Hydrated appearance / pores | Cosmetic appearance may change without proving persistent collagen gain |
| Lifting / major laxity | Do not import focused-ultrasound or RF outcomes; compare the [HIFU](../11_hifu_skin_tightening/index.html) and [RF](../07_radio_frequency_skin_tightening/index.html) evidence separately |
| Pigment / PIH | No dedicated efficacy verdict from this initial corpus; see [pigment strategy](../01_ipl_hair_removal/index.html#doc7) and [body-pigment evidence](../22_body_hyperpigmentation_long_term/index.html) |

## Evidence gaps

Obtain dated IFUs, regulatory identifiers, calibration/service requirements, adverse-event data, real local treatment quotes, and exact-model home/OEM human trials before upgrading this page into a buying guide.
